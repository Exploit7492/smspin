Temporary Aukro Number vs Virtual Number - What's the Real Difference?

People often use "temporary" and "virtual" interchangeably, but they're not the same thing. A virtual number is a permanent, cloud-based line you can keep for months. A temporary Aukro number is disposable: you use it once, get the OTP, and it's done. For Aukro account verification, temporary wins because it's cheaper and leaves no trace.

  • Lifespan: Temporary = minutes to hours; virtual = days to years.
  • Cost: Temporary numbers cost a fraction of a virtual number for a one-time need.
  • Use case: Temporary for OTP-only verification; virtual for ongoing calls and texts.
  • Reusability: Temporary numbers are usually single-use; virtual numbers can be recycled.

Security experts generally agree that SMS-based 2FA is better than nothing, but it's not the strongest form of authentication. That's exactly why you want a disposable number: it limits the attack surface. Your main SIM stays clean, and the temporary line vanishes after use.
