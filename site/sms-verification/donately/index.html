Why Codes Fail – Troubleshooting Donately OTP Receiving

When OTP receiving fails, it's usually because of number exhaustion (used too many times), carrier filtering, or a mismatch between the number's origin and your account region. First, try a fresh number from a different country or area code. If the problem persists, check whether your provider explicitly supports donation platforms or if the number is flagged as "high-risk."

  • Number exhaustion: The same virtual number has been requested too many times by different users, leading to Donately blocking it.

  • Carrier filtering: Some mobile operators block SMS from known virtual number aggregators, perceiving them as spam sources.

  • Region mismatch: Donately may not send codes to numbers from certain countries, or it might flag numbers that don't match the IP address region.
