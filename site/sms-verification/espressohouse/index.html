Why Isn’t Espresso House Sending Me My Verification Code?

When Espresso House SMS delivery fails, it's almost always due to one of three things: carrier blocklisting, number portability delays, or the app's own timeout rules. The system typically sends the code once, then blocks repeated requests to prevent brute-force attacks. If your number was recently ported or sits on a VoIP carrier, Espresso House may silently drop the SMS.

Here's what's actually happening behind the scenes:

  • Carrier Blacklists: Some mobile networks flag Espresso House verification codes as promotional traffic and block them. Your carrier might not even tell you.

  • Portability Issues: Just switched carriers? It can take 24–48 hours for SMS services to sync fully. Codes sent during that window often vanish.

  • App Timeout: Request a code too fast or too many times, and the "Resend" button locks for 60+ minutes. It's a brute-force protection, but it's annoying.

  • Silent Failure: Sometimes, Espresso House shows "Code Sent" but never actually transmits the SMS. This usually happens when the number has a suspicious history or is flagged as VoIP.

The Espresso House Mobile App: Why SMS Codes Fail at Login

The Espresso House app relies on carrier-grade SMS gateways, and those gateways aren't always reliable for international numbers. If you're travelling or using a number outside the app's supported region, the code may not appear. The app also sometimes caches login sessions, causing a mismatch between the server's code and your app's timer.

Here's what specifically causes app-related failures:

  • Regional Restrictions: Espresso House prioritises SMS delivery for numbers in its primary regions. Swedish (+46) numbers usually work best. Numbers from other countries may face delays or get blocked entirely.

  • Session Caching: The app might hold onto old session data, interfering with new verification requests. Force-quit the app and reopen it to reset the flow.

  • Network Handoff: Switching from Wi-Fi to cellular data mid-request can route the SMS into a dead queue where it'll never be delivered

  • App Version Bug: Older versions of the app sometimes have known bugs that affect SMS delivery. Always update to the latest version.

Espresso House Code Not Arriving? Check These 3 Settings First

Before giving up, check these three settings. Do Not Disturb (DND) mode on your phone often blocks unknown senders, including verification codes. SMS filtering apps like Truecaller or Google Messages may automatically quarantine the OTP. And carrier spam filters can block shortcode messages. Call your carrier and ask them to allow Espresso House's sender ID.

Here's what to check in detail:

  • Do Not Disturb (DND) Mode: Make sure DND or Focus Modes (iOS) and Do Not Disturb settings (Android) aren't active. They can suppress notifications from unknown numbers.

  • SMS Filtering Apps: Check your messaging app's "Spam," "Blocked," or "Archive" folders. Apps like Truecaller, Google Messages, or Samsung Messages sometimes misclassify OTPs as spam.

  • Carrier Spam Filters: Mobile carriers use aggressive spam filters that can block shortcode SMS. In the US, you might need to ask your carrier to unblock "shortcode SMS." In India, you might need to request a "promotional SMS" allowance

  • SIM Card Issues: An old or damaged SIM can prevent shortcode reception. Try inserting your SIM into a different phone to rule out device-specific problems.

Espresso House App Phone Verification Problems: Device vs Network Issues

Phone verification failures usually split evenly between device bugs and network problems. On the device side, outdated app versions, full message inboxes, or disabled SMS permissions can stop codes from showing. On the network side, carrier firewalls or roaming agreements may intercept the SMS. Test your device first by receiving an SMS from a friend, then test the network by switching to a different carrier's SIM.

How to tell the difference:

  • Device Fixes:

    • Update the Espresso House app to the latest version.

    • Check SMS permissions: Settings > Apps > [Espresso House App] and verify SMS permissions are enabled

    • Clearing old messages and a full inbox can prevent new ones from arriving.

  • Network Fixes:

    • Switch networks, borrow a friend's SIM to see if the issue is carrier-specific

    • If roaming internationally, your home carrier might block international shortcode SMS. Enable Wi-Fi calling to avoid this.

    • If you have a dual-SIM phone, make sure the Espresso House app uses the SIM that supports SMS reception.
