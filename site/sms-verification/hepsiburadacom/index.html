FAQ

Common questions answered

Is using a temporary number for Hepsiburada legal and safe?+

Yes, using a temporary number is legal in most jurisdictions, as long as you're not violating Hepsiburada's terms of service. It's a privacy tool: your real number stays off their database. However, using any verification method to compromise account security controls for fraud, like creating fake seller accounts or abusing referral bonuses, violates both the law and Hepsiburada's policies.

Why do Hepsiburada verification codes fail to arrive, even after resending?+

Codes typically fail because your carrier blocks Hepsiburada's automated sender ID as spam, or because the SMS gateway has a regional issue with your network. Resending won't fix a carrier-level filter. A different phone number from a different carrier is the most reliable way to break that loop.

Should I use a one-time number or rent a number for Hepsiburada?+

Use a one-time number if you only need to verify a login or create a single account. Rent a number for 24 hours or longer if you expect multiple verification codes, such as when setting up a seller account that requires repeated phone checks. Renting is cost-effective only when you exceed a certain verification volume.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for?+

Do not use temporary numbers for banking, government identity verification, or any service where losing access to the number means losing access to your funds or data. Also avoid using them to create multiple accounts to avoid platform restrictions that's abuse and can result in bans or legal liability.

How do I fix an "OTP timeout" error on Hepsiburada?+

An OTP timeout happens when you don't enter the code before its expiry window, typically 3 to 5 minutes. Refresh the page, request a fresh code, and keep the SMS dashboard open in a second window so you can copy the code immediately. If codes keep expiring instantly, the issue is your SMS delivery speed, not your typing speed.

Can I receive a Hepsiburada code via WhatsApp or another messaging app?+

Hepsiburada officially only sends codes via SMS, email, or automated phone calls, not through WhatsApp, Telegram, or other messaging platforms. Any third-party service claiming to deliver codes over messaging apps is either unofficial or uses a SIM-based backend, which can violate Hepsiburada's terms.

What happens if the temporary number expires before I finish verifying?+

If your number expires mid-verification, you lose access to the OTP and must purchase a new number to start over. This is why choosing a rental window that covers your full verification timeline matters more than picking the cheapest per-use option.

Why Didn't I Get My Hepsiburada SMS Verification Code?

Hepsiburada SMS codes usually fail because of carrier filtering, poor signal, or a full SMS inbox. The quickest fix is to hit the "Resend" button, but if that doesn't work after two or three tries, the issue is likely with your network, not the app, meaning no number of resends will solve it. If you're stuck in this loop, a temporary number from a different network is often the fastest path to a fresh code.

  • Carrier-Level Spam Filters: Many carriers, especially prepaid SIMs and smaller regional networks, automatically block automated SMS senders like Hepsiburada. The message never reaches your phone.

  • Full Inbox or Blocked Permissions: If your SMS inbox is full or you've accidentally blocked the sender ID, the code silently fails to store.

  • Session Locks: Hepsiburada throttles resend requests. Spamming the button can trigger a temporary ban on resending, usually lasting around 10 minutes.

  • Roaming Issues: If you're travelling, your home SIM's roaming settings often fail to deliver local shortcodes; this is a known technical limitation.

If the SMS gateway isn't delivering to your specific carrier, pressing "Resend" 20 times won't change the outcome. You're repeatedly trying a broken route. For context, while SMS is common, regulators like NIST recommend moving away from SMS where possible for higher-security accounts. Hepsiburada Call Verification Option: Does It Work for OTPs?

Hepsiburada does offer a call verification option in certain account-recovery flows, where an automated system reads the code aloud instead of sending a text. This is useful when SMS is down, but it has a key limitation: you must answer the call from the exact number on file, and in most cases it can't be forwarded to a VoIP line. If you don't have access to a trusted phone line or your carrier fails to connect the call, neither SMS nor call verification will work.

  • When it appears: The call option is usually presented after two failed SMS attempts, not as a first-line choice.

  • Caller ID: The automated call is typically generated from a Turkish or regional number; spam filters on some carriers can block it.

  • Timing: You cannot schedule or request a call-back window; the system calls immediately after you select the option.

  • Availability: If call verification isn't available in your country, Hepsiburada may only show SMS or email methods.
