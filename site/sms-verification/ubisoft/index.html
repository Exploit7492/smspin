FAQ

Common questions answered

Why does Ubisoft SMS verification fail?+

Ubisoft SMS verification usually fails because of carrier blocks, VoIP number detection, international SMS delays, restricted regions, or numbers previously linked to flagged accounts.

What error messages can appear during Ubisoft SMS verification?+

Common messages include “Invalid phone number,” “Verification failed,” and “SMS not delivered.” These usually mean that Ubisoft’s SMS system cannot validate or deliver to the number being used.

Why is my real phone number not receiving Ubisoft codes?+

Your real number may fail if your carrier blocks shortcode SMS, your number is on a prepaid or MVNO network, you are roaming internationally, or the SMS arrives after Ubisoft’s verification window expires.

Does Ubisoft accept VoIP numbers?+

Usually, no. Ubisoft often rejects VoIP or virtual numbers from services like Google Voice, Skype, TextNow, and free SMS receiver websites because they are not tied to real mobile carriers.

Why do free SMS receiver sites fail for Ubisoft?+

Most free SMS receiver sites use recycled VoIP numbers. Ubisoft can quickly detect and block these numbers, which often cause verification failures.

What should I try first if my Ubisoft SMS code is not arriving?+

Try restarting your phone, switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, checking SMS spam filters, clearing your inbox, and requesting the code again after about 10 minutes.

Can my carrier block Ubisoft verification texts?+

Yes. Some carriers, especially prepaid carriers and MVNOs, may block shortcode SMS. You can contact your carrier and ask whether they support Ubisoft shortcode messages.

What Is Ubisoft SMS Verification and Why Does It Keep Failing?

Ubisoft uses SMS verification to lock down your account during login, password recovery, or account sign-up. You get a one-time code on your phone. Seems simple, right? But Ubisoft SMS verification fails more often than you'd expect, creating a sticky situation.

The real reasons? Carrier restrictions, international roaming hiccups, and this is a big one: Ubisoft’s system detecting VoIP numbers. Knowing exactly why it fails is the first step to fixing it.

Common Ubisoft SMS Verification Error Codes and Messages

You might see "Invalid phone number," "Verification failed," or "SMS not delivered." Those errors pop up when your number doesn't match what Ubisoft expects. Often, it's a technical mismatch between your carrier and Ubisoft’s SMS gateway. Not a nice experience.

Why Your Real Number Fails Ubisoft’s SMS Verification

Your real number can fail your own number just because your carrier does not support shortcode SMS. Many prepaid carriers and MVNOs (mobile virtual network operators) don't route those messages. And if you're roaming internationally, your home carrier might not deliver the SMS in time, so the code expires.

Ubisoft SMS Verification Not Working: 5 Most Likely Causes

If you're staring at the "SMS not delivered" screen, it's usually one of five things: your carrier blocks shortcodes, Ubisoft flags your number as VoIP, your number is from a restricted region, international routing causes delays, or your number was previously used on a flagged account. Each has a fix.

Carrier and Network Blocks

Some mobile networks (especially prepaid or MVNOs) just straight-up block Ubisoft’s shortcodes. The code never reaches your phone. Your best bet? Contact your carrier and ask if they support shortcode SMS for Ubisoft.

VoLTE, VoIP, and Virtual Number Detection

Ubisoft sniffs out VoIP numbers like Google Voice, Skype, or TextNow. They flag those numbers because they're not tied to a real mobile carrier. If you grab a free SMS site number, Ubisoft will almost certainly say "no."

International SMS Delivery Delays

International SMS can take 5 to 30 minutes. Ubisoft’s verification window is short. If you're outside your number's home country, the SMS arrives too late and times out. That's a common "SMS not delivered" cause.

How to Fix Ubisoft Text Verification Not Working (Step-by-Step)

Before reaching for a third-party service, try these first: restart your phone, switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, clear SMS spam filters, or request the code again after 10 minutes. Sometimes simple things work. If not, check with your carrier whether they pass Ubisoft SMS. Some carriers do; others don't.

Troubleshooting Your Current Phone Number

  • Toggle mobile data on/off or switch Wi-Fi networks.

  • Clear your SMS inbox spam filter; it might be hiding the code.

  • Request the code again after a 10-minute delay (Ubisoft resends after timeout).

  • Contact your carrier and confirm they support shortcode SMS.

  • If roaming, enable international SMS manually or grab a temporary local SIM.

Switching to a Compatible Carrier or Region

If your carrier blocks Ubisoft SMS, you have two choices: switch to a carrier that supports shortcodes (like T-Mobile or Vodafone), or use a temporary number from a country known for reliable delivery,   the USA or UK often work best.
