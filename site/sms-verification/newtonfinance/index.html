What to Do When NewtonFinance SMS Code Never Arrives (Real Troubleshooting)

“Spam the resend button” is the natural, lizard-brain reaction, but in the world of SMS verification, it’s a self-destruct button. NewtonFinance’s systems watch for rapid-fire requests as a telltale sign of a bot or a SIM-swap attack. So, slow your roll and work the problem logically.
