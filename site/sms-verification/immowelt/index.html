Common Immowelt Phone Verification Errors   And What They Actually Mean

"Invalid number," "code expired," "verification failed"   these Immowelt phone verification errors each point to a different root cause, and they're not always what they seem. Most errors fall into three buckets: carrier issues, code input problems, or regional restrictions. Once you know which one you're dealing with, the fix becomes obvious.

Here's a quick breakdown of the most common error messages:

  • "Invalid number": The platform's format check is failing; possibly the country code is missing, or you're using an unsupported carrier prefix.

  • "Code expired": You took too long between receiving the SMS and entering it; the code is dead on arrival.

  • "Verification failed": Often means the number has been flagged, possibly previously used on another account or flagged by fraud systems.

  • "SMS not delivered": This is a carrier-level failure; the message was sent but never arrived, often due to SMS filtering.

  • "Number already in use": The number is tied to another Immowelt account; you can't reuse it.

Most of these are preventable. You fix format errors by checking the country code. You can fix expired codes by acting faster. But "SMS not delivered" is the frustrating one; it's completely out of your control because your carrier is silently blocking the message. That's when you need to switch to a number that has a proven delivery route.
