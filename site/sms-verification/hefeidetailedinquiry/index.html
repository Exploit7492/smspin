Is using a virtual SMS verification service legal?+

Yes, for legitimate reasons: privacy protection, dev testing, spam prevention. It crosses into illegal or unethical territory only when used for fraud, mass fake accounts, or deliberate terms-of-service violations. Check your local regulations regardless.

Why does my code from Telegram or WhatsApp never arrive?+

Usually, the app has blocked that number’s range or your IP looks suspicious. Grab a fresh number from a different country; switch from India to the US, for instance and kill any VPN that might be flagging your request.

What’s the difference between a one-time number and a rental number?+

A one-time number handles a single verification, then disappears. A rental number stays dedicated to you for days or up to a month, ideal for account recovery or apps that demand a persistent number for 2FA.

Can I use virtual numbers for banking or financial apps?+

Rarely. High-security financial institutions want physical SIMs and in-person KYC. They aggressively block virtual numbers, which is why we label them “unsupported” outright.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for?+

Don’t use them for scam accounts, platform-rule violations, mass-registering for referral fraud, or accessing illegal services. Those activities break platform rules and may be criminal.

What happens if I never receive the code?+

If the failure is on our end—an infrastructure error or carrier issue—you get an automatic refund per our documented policy, with no support ticket required.

How do I troubleshoot a failed verification beyond switching numbers?+

First, confirm in our directory that the app supports virtual numbers. Then clear your browser cache and cookies, double-check that you typed the number correctly, and wait at least 90 seconds before retrying to avoid tripping app-side spam filters.
