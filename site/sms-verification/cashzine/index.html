Why didn’t I receive my CashZine OTP?+

The most common reasons are: the number was already tied to another CashZine account, your SMS provider's numbers are in a blocked range, or the app's 60–120 second timer expired before the carrier delivered the code. Try a fresh number from a different country, and use a service that refunds if no code arrives.

What’s the difference between a one-time number and a rental number for CashZine?+

A one-time number is for a single verification. You use it, get the code, and never use it again. A rental number remains active for 1–30 days, making it ideal for ongoing account access (such as daily logins). Rentals cost slightly more but save you from re-verifying each time.

Can I use a free temporary number for CashZine?+

Possibly, but free numbers are often blocked by apps like CashZine because hundreds of others have used them. Paid services with fresh number pools have dramatically higher success rates. It's worth a few cents to avoid the headache of repeated failures.

What shouldn’t I use a temporary number for?+

Never use temporary numbers for banking, government services (like tax or ID verification), or password resets on sensitive accounts like email or social media. These services often require long-term access to the number, and losing it could lock you out of your account.

How long does CashZine usually take to send the OTP?+

Most OTPs arrive within 30–60 seconds. If you haven't received anything after 90 seconds, the carrier may have blocked the message. Request a new number and try again; this usually resolves the issue.
