Is it legal to resell HIBOX SMS verification numbers?+

Yes, as long as you are helping users verify their own account. You must not create fake accounts or violate HIBOX’s Terms of Service. Keep it to legitimate use cases only. When in doubt, don’t.

A client's code isn't arriving. What do you do?+

First, check if the request is still open in your dashboard. Orders should be fulfilled within 2-3 minutes. If it doesn't, most reseller providers will auto-refund the order, and you can try once with a new SMS number. Don’t panic; pivot.

Is it better to sell one-time OTPs or longer rentals?+

Start with one-time, 60-second verification. It’s simple and fastest. Later, you can offer long-term rental options (from one day up to a month) for clients who need reliable "logged-in" HIBOX. Start simple, then expand.

When should you not sell a temp number?+

Don't accept orders. Make a simple policy: if it involves a financial transaction(like a bank app or PayPal), turn down the request. Fraud prevention starts with you. Your reputation is worth more than one sale.

What if someone claims they "never got the WhatsApp code"?+

That's a provider issue, not just the country. Check in the dashboard. If there is no code, there is no charge; customers are auto-credited, and you're no longer the bad guy. Let the system do the talking.

Do I need to "ouchy" "success to a credit card" to start?+

You'll start with a prepaid top-up(up to $10+) from cards or crypto. The money stays with the provider and reflects in your wallet as buying power. It’s not a recurring charge; it’s a wallet you control.

Troubleshooting and Best Practices for HIBOX SMS Reselling

Common mistakes happen when using temporary numbers. Here’s how to handle them smoothly because it’s not about avoiding problems; it’s about handling them well:

  • Mistake 1: Screaming 'not in stock'. You can check your panel in real time. Better to say "out of stock" for the requested number but offer a German alternative. Your provider's dashboard shows you exactly what you can fulfill. Don’t guess; check.

  • Mistake 2: Confusion about returns. Set your policy. If the code hasn't arrived in 3 minutes, allow a full refund and move to the next order. Your provider will cover you (auto-refund) when the code isn't delivered. Clear refund policies build trust.
