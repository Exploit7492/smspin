Farmiso Verification Gateway vs Direct SMS: Which Approach Wins?

A Farmiso verification gateway routes your OTP through an intermediary platform rather than directly to your personal SIM. For most use cases, the gateway wins hands down. Gateways like SMSPin deliver clean numbers, real-time delivery, and the flexibility to swap numbers if one fails. Direct SMS to your real phone is simpler, sure — but it ties your personal number to Farmiso forever and exposes you to spam and data leakage.

Direct SMS means one number, one account, and no swapping if the code fails. Your personal number is now permanently in Farmiso's database. The gateway approach gives you multiple fresh numbers on demand, instant replacement on failure, and zero exposure of your real identity.

Gateway wins on privacy, scalability, and recovery speed when codes fail. Direct SMS wins only on simplicity — no extra platform, no cost per use. But that simplicity comes with a privacy cost that's hard to ignore.
