Why Dave SMS Verification Can Be Tricky (And What Usually Goes Wrong)

Dave's SMS verification system is built to ping real, active SIM cards. That's great for security, but it creates friction when your main number is already in use, on a do-not-call list, or you want to keep your personal line out of the loop.

The most common headache? The app flags virtual numbers from low-quality providers, and you end up staring at a "Dave SMS code not sent" error. Here's the thing: it's usually not your phone. It's the reputation of the number you're using.

Here's what typically goes wrong:

  • Carrier restrictions: Some mobile carriers block SMS from financial apps like Dave, treating them as suspicious.

  • App-specific blocks: Dave likely maintains a list of known VoIP or temporary number pools it refuses to accept. This is standard practice for financial apps following industry best practices.
