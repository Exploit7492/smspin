Buying a virtual number — questions

What is a virtual number used for?

Receiving the one-time verification SMS an app sends when you sign up or log in, without handing that app your real mobile number. The number is issued to you alone for the length of one code, then released.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the country and the app, because supply does. Open the country you need and the page lists the live price for every app we have numbers in stock for — that price is what checkout charges, not an indicative rate card.

What if the code never arrives?

You are refunded in full, automatically, back to your SMSPin balance. No ticket, no request. Nothing is charged unless a code is actually delivered to you.

Do I need to give you my own phone number?

No. An email address and a balance are all that is required — the entire point of the product is that your real number never touches the app you are verifying.

Can I keep a number for longer than one code?

Yes, that is a rental rather than a one-time number: it stays yours for days or weeks and receives unlimited SMS. Rentals are priced per day and live under /rent.
