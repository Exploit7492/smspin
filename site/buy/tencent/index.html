Why Your Free QQ Verification Code Fails and How to Fix It

Even with a reliable service, QQ verification codes can fail to arrive, and it's rarely the number's fault. Tencent sometimes blocks numbers from known virtual providers, or the OTP expires before you enter it. The fix is simple: try a fresh number, make sure you're not reusing one that's already been flagged, and confirm the country you picked matches the number's region.

Common failure causes include:

  • Expired codes: OTPs typically expire within a few minutes. Enter them promptly.
  • Flagged numbers: Tencent may block numbers that have been used too many times.
  • Country mismatches: If the number's country doesn't match your selected region, delivery fails.
  • Network delays: Rare, but SMS routing can occasionally be slow.

When a code genuinely doesn't arrive, SMSPin's auto-refund policy covers you. You get your money back without filing a claim.
