Afterpay Verification Code Not Coming Here’s How to Fix It

TL;DR

Not receiving your Afterpay verification code? Learn the most common reasons OTPs fail and the practical steps you can take to restore SMS delivery, troubleshoot network issues, and complete verification successfully.

Let's be real most of these failures boil down to three things: carrier filters, phone settings, or a bogged-down SMS gateway. Afterpay sends OTPs via standard SMS, so if your carrier flags those shortcode messages as spam or your inbox is full, the code vanishes. Other times, the app times out before the SMS even lands.

  • Carrier-level spam filters that block shortcode SMS from financial services.

  • Phone settings like Do Not Disturb or Silence Unknown Callers that suppress OTP messages.

  • Temporary Afterpay server delays during high-traffic periods (e.g., payday weekends).

  • Using a VoIP or Google Voice number that Afterpay’s system rejects by default.

#Quick Answer:

  • Check your phone settings first: Disable Do Not Disturb, restart your device, and confirm SMS is working with another service.

  • Wait 2–3 minutes before resending: Rapid resend requests trigger Afterpay's rate limit and won't speed up delivery.

  • Use a real SIM-based virtual number if blocked: VoIP numbers don't work choose a temporary number from a supported country.

  • Contact support as a last resort: Provide a list of steps you've already tried to speed up manual verification.

#Quick Device & Network Checks Before You Panic

Don't jump into account settings yet. First, do the basics: turn off airplane mode, toggle mobile data, and make sure your SMS inbox isn't full. Travelling abroad? Confirm you can receive SMS while roaming. I've seen a phone restart fix more mysterious OTP failures than you'd expect.

  • Restart your phone to clear temporary network glitches.

  • Check whether you can receive SMS from other services (e.g., banks, delivery apps) to isolate the issue.

  • Disable Wi-Fi calling temporarily it can interfere with SMS routing.

  • Make sure your phone number is entered correctly in Afterpay (country code included).

#How to Force Afterpay to Resend the Verification Code

Afterpay usually gives you a Resend code button after 30–60 seconds. If it doesn't appear, close the app completely and restart the flow. Don't spam that button. Each request resets the OTP's validity window, and rapid clicks can temporarily lock you out.

  • Close the Afterpay app from your recent apps menu, then relaunch.

  • Tap Resend code only after the visible countdown completes.

  • Wait 5 minutes between attempts to avoid triggering Afterpay's rate-limit protections.

  • If using a browser, clear cookies or open a new window in incognito mode before retrying.

#How to Reset or Recover a Stuck Afterpay Verification Process

If Afterpay stubbornly refuses to send a new code, you can reset the verification session. Log out completely, or force-close the registration page and start fresh. In the app, go to your profile, tap Account, then Verify phone number to reinitialize your phone number. This clears any stuck session that's blocking things.

  • Log out of Afterpay completely, then sign back in to reset the verification session.

  • If you can't log out, clear the app's cache in your phone settings (Android: Settings > Apps > Afterpay > Storage > Clear Cache).

  • For web users: log out, clear browser cache, and reload the verification page.

  • Do not attempt more than 3 resets within an hour to avoid temporary account locks.

#What to Do When Afterpay Says Account Verification Failed

That dreaded error usually means your number doesn't meet Afterpay's security standards. Maybe it's a VoIP line, an improperly registered prepaid number, or a carrier flagged for high fraud risk. This isn't a permanent ban it's just Afterpay being cautious. The fix? Try a different, more reliable number.

  • Check that your phone number is a standard mobile number, not a VoIP or landline.

  • Ensure the number is active and not suspended for non-payment.

  • If you recently ported your number, wait 24–48 hours for carrier databases to update.

  • Try verifying with a trusted friend's number as a temporary workaround while you sort out your own.

#Why Afterpay SMS Codes Get Delayed (And How to Speed Them Up)

Normally, Afterpay OTPs arrive within 10–30 seconds. But during peak hours evenings, weekends, paydays SMS gateways can get congested, and your carrier might throttle bulk messages. The smart play is to wait 2–3 minutes before hitting resend. Each new request puts you back in the queue.

  • Carrier SMS throttling during network congestion (common on MVNOs like Mint or Visible).

  • Afterpay's SMS provider is experiencing regional delays (check Downdetector for your area).

  • Phone's SMS filtering apps (like Truecaller) intercept OTPs and put them in a spam folder.

  • International roaming delays if your home carrier's SMS routing is slow.

#Still Not Getting the Code? When to Try a Different Phone Number

If you've run through everything and the issue of the Afterpay verification code not coming persists, the problem is likely your phone number. Numbers flagged in carrier databases, prepaid lines, or those used for too many failed verifications can get blocked by Afterpay's fraud detection. Switching numbers often solves this instantly.

  • Borrow a family member's number temporarily to confirm the issue is with your line, not Afterpay.

  • If you have a dual-SIM phone, switch the verification to your second line.

  • Consider using a fresh virtual number (more on this below) that isn't tied to previous failed attempts.

  • Do not use a Google Voice or Skype number Afterpay blocks most VoIP-based numbers.

#How to Prevent Afterpay Verification Issues in the Future

Once you're verified, a few simple habits can keep things smooth: keep your carrier account active, don't switch numbers often, and avoid hammering the verification button. Also, make sure your phone isn't auto-deleting sms verification from unknown senders.

  • Stick with one consistent phone number for your Afterpay account to build trust in their system.

  • Turn off SMS-blocking apps or allowlist Afterpay's shortcode (if your carrier supports it).

  • Update your Afterpay contact number immediately if you change carriers or lines.

  • Avoid using Afterpay on rooted phones or emulators, which can trigger additional verification checks.

#When to Contact Afterpay Support (And What to Tell Them)

If you've done all the troubleshooting and the code still won't come, it's time to call in the cavalry. Before contacting support, gather your account email, the phone number you're trying to verify, and a list of every step you've already tried. They can check if your number is blocked or if there's a server glitch. But be warned manual verification can take 24–48 hours.

  • Contact via Afterpay's in-app chat for the fastest response (avoid email for urgent issues).

  • Say: I've tried network resets, app cache clears, and waited 5 minutes between resends still no OTP.

  • Ask support to check if your number is on any internal blocklist or rate-limit queue.

  • If they can't resolve it via SMS, request an alternative verification method (email or in-app prompt) if available.

#FAQ

Why is my Afterpay verification code not coming?

The most common reasons include carrier SMS filtering, temporary network issues, incorrect phone number formatting, an outdated Afterpay app, or delays with Afterpay's SMS provider. Check your device settings, confirm your number is correct, and wait a few minutes before requesting another code.

How long does it take to receive an Afterpay verification code?

Most verification codes arrive within 10–30 seconds, though delays of 2–3 minutes can occur during busy periods or due to carrier network congestion.

Why does Afterpay say my account verification failed?

This message can appear if you're using a VoIP number, the system has flagged your phone number, the number was recently ported to a new carrier, or there were multiple failed verification attempts. Using a valid mobile number usually resolves the issue.

Can I use a virtual phone number for Afterpay verification?

Some virtual numbers may work, while others may be rejected depending on Afterpay's security policies. Success depends on the provider and the type of number, so there's no guarantee that every virtual number will be accepted.

How many times can I request an Afterpay verification code?

Afterpay limits repeated verification requests to prevent abuse. Requesting codes too frequently may trigger a temporary cooldown, so it's best to wait a few minutes between attempts and use only the most recent code.

What should I do if my Afterpay verification code keeps expiring?

If your code expires before you can enter it, request a new one and use it immediately. Avoid requesting multiple codes in quick succession, as each new request invalidates the previous code.

When should I contact Afterpay Support?

If you've verified your phone number, checked your network and device settings, waited between resend attempts, and still aren't receiving verification codes, contact Afterpay Support. Be ready to provide your account email, phone number, device details, and the troubleshooting steps you've already completed to help resolve the issue faster.
