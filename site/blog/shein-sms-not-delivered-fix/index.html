#Why Is SHEIN Not Sending Me a Verification Code? The Honest Truth

Here's the thing: SHEIN's SMS delivery usually isn't broken; it's silently blocked. The platform relies on automated SMS gateways that carriers and spam filters frequently flag, especially when you're re-requesting after a failed attempt. If your number sits on a shared carrier plan, a prepaid SIM with strict filters, or a VOIP line that's been blacklisted, SHEIN's system will quietly fail to deliver without ever telling you why.

  • Carriers may block or filter short-code SMS, including five-digit numbers, from international retailers because of spam protection policies.

  • SHEIN's anti-fraud system may limit repeated OTP requests, so rapid retries can temporarily affect verification.

  • If you previously used a number that has since been recycled, activity associated with the previous owner may sometimes affect verification.

  • SIM registration mismatches, including differences between the registered name and phone number in some regions, may contribute to carrier-side rejection.

The result can be a sent status on SHEIN's end while the verification message never appears in your inbox. This disconnect can make error messages difficult to interpret. SMS delivery can also vary because of carrier filtering, routing, and other network factors. #SHEIN Code Not Coming Through? First, Check These 4 Things

Before you blame SHEIN, run through this 30-second checklist. It'll eliminate the most common culprits fast. Make sure your phone has a strong signal, not just Wi-Fi; disable any SMS-blocking or call-filtering apps; verify you're entering the correct international format; and confirm your inbox isn't silently filing passcodes into a Promotions or Spam folder. We've seen people stuck for an hour because their phone flagged the code as spam.

  • Toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds to force your device to reconnect to the carrier network.

#SHEIN SMS Delivery Failed: Your Carrier Might Be the Problem, Not SHEIN

Short-code SMS, the five-digit numbers SHEIN uses, relies entirely on your carrier's routing agreements. If you're on a budget MVNO, a resold SIM, or a number ported multiple times, your carrier may have deprioritized or outright blocked short-code delivery. The text never reaches your phone, and SHEIN's system logs it as sent, a dead end for both sides.

  • MVNOs: Budget carriers that piggyback on major networks often lack direct short-code agreements.

  • Ported numbers: Switched carriers sometimes lose short-code routing during the transition window.

  • Some carriers block international SMS by default: Confirm with customer support.

  • Business or enterprise SIMs: These frequently block OTP messages for security compliance.

The CTIA Short Code Monitoring Handbook, the industry standard for short-code governance, notes that carriers have discretion over which short codes they allow on their networks. If SHEIN's code isn't on your carrier's approved list, delivery drops silently every time. That's why the SMS works for your friend on the same network but not for you; it's a contract issue, not a technical one.

#SHEIN Verification Code Expired Before Delivery? Here's the Time Limit Trap

SHEIN's OTP has a short validity window, typically 10 minutes, but that clock starts the moment the code is generated, not when it arrives. If your carrier delays delivery by routing internationally, or if you missed a notification, you'll hit code expired before you even read it. The system won't resend; it forces a fresh request, which can trigger another throttle.

  • The 10-minute window is standard, but carrier routing delays can eat into it without you knowing.

  • Requesting a new code invalidates the previous one instantly, even if it later arrives.

  • Lock screen notifications that don't populate correctly can cause you to miss the SMS before expiry.

  • If you're in a poor coverage area, the code may arrive after the window closes, showing as expired.

The expiration message is misleading because it implies you waited too long. In reality, your carrier was the bottleneck. If you see expired on a code you never saw, your carrier is the hinge; switching numbers or delivery paths is the only way around it.

If your primary number's carrier is blocking short codes, inserting a different phone number on the verification screen is the fastest documented workaround.   • Use a number from a different carrier if possible to avoid repeating the same carrier block.

  • Don't reuse the same phone within 24 hours; SHEIN flags repeated numbers.

Think of this as a reset button for the OTP pipeline. SHEIN's anti-fraud system tracks the phone number, not your account, so a new number clears the throttle and starts fresh.
