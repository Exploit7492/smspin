This guide is for anyone hitting a wall with Wise verification. Whether it's already taken, a failed code, or you can't seem to add your number anywhere we've got fixes that actually work.

#Why Does Wise Say My Phone Number Is Already Taken?

Here's the thing: Wise links one phone number to one account. Period. If you see already taken, either someone else used that number before you (common with recycled SIMs), or you already have a Wise account hiding somewhere in your email history. It's not a glitch it's anti-fraud logic that sometimes catches the wrong people.

That phone number already taken message means Wise's system has already seen your number.

  • Recycled carrier numbers: Your new SIM may have previously belonged to someone with a Wise account so that the number may be linked to their profile. Free numbers can have similar history issues if they’ve been reused. 

  • Multi-Account Restriction: Wise says one phone number per account. If you signed up years ago and forgot, or tried making a second account, that number's already flagged.

  • Typo or Second-Hand Number: A fat-fingered typo during signup? That can accidentally tie you to someone else's number. Same deal if you bought a prepaid SIM second-hand the original owner may have verified Wise with it.

  • KYC Logic: This isn't malicious. It's Know Your Customer logic designed to stop fraud and prevent people from gaming the system by creating multiple accounts.

#Wise Cannot Use Phone Number – What This Error Actually Means.

Cannot use phone number is usually about format or type issues, not duplication. The country code might be wrong, or you're using a number type Wise doesn't accept.

This error points to a technical mismatch, not a duplicate account.

  • Country Code Mismatch: Classic mistake entering 07700 without +447700 or using the wrong prefix entirely. You might've typed +1 for a UK number instead of +44, or just forgot the + entirely.

  • VoIP or Virtual Number Restrictions: Wise blocks certain VoIP ranges (e.g., Google Voice, TextNow) from registration. They don't consider those secure enough for money stuff.

  • Number Porting Lag: Just ported your number to a new carrier? Wise might still see it under the old one, causing a temporary block.

  • Temporary Glitch: Sometimes Wise's SMS gateway just hiccups. Wait 15 minutes and try again it's that simple sometimes.

#Wise Phone Verification Failed – Why Codes Don't Arrive and What to Do

Verification codes fail because of network delays, carrier filtering, or Wise's anti-spam limits. No code within 5 minutes? DO NOT spam Resend that locks you out for up to an hour.

Here's why those codes ghost you:

  • Carrier Filtering: Some mobile networks block SMS messages from international short codes or automated systems. Common with certain carriers and prepaid plans.

  • Fix: Try Wi-Fi Calling if available, or swap SIM cards.

  • Wise's 5-Minute Cooldown/Rate Limiting: Click Resend Code too many times and Wise gets suspicious. It'll trigger a temporary block for anywhere from 30 minutes to a few hours.

  • Fix: Stop clicking. Wait at least 30 minutes, maybe a couple of hours, before trying again.

  • SMS Forwarding Conflicts: If your phone forwards messages to another device or to email, the code might be hidden there instead of in your inbox.

  • Network Delays or Poor Signal: Weak signal or congestion can delay SMS delivery.

  • Fix: Move somewhere with better reception or restart your phone.

  • VoIP/Virtual Number Issues: Wise may not send codes to VoIP numbers at all.

  • Voice Call Option: Look for Receive code via voice call it can sometimes bypass SMS issues.

Codes still not coming? Many people switch to services offering fresh temporary numbers for initial verification. These often have better luck bypassing carrier filtering.

#Wise Cannot Add Phone Number – Clearing Cache, Region Locks, and App Glitches.

Adding a phone number in Wise may fail because of cached app data or session conflicts. Clear the app cache, reinstall if needed, and check that your device region matches your billing country before trying to receive SMS again.

Try these common fixes:

  • Clear App Cache:

  • Android: Settings > Apps > Wise > Storage > Clear Cache. Don't clear data unless you're ready to log in again.

  • iOS: No direct clear cache here. Offload the app (Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Wise > Offload App) or delete and reinstall.

  • Check Region Locks/VPNs: Are you using a VPN that doesn't match your registered address? Wise may block sensitive actions, such as adding a number.

  • Fix: Disable your VPN or match the region to your billing country.

  • App Version: Make sure your Wise app is up to date. Old versions can have bugs that break validation.

  • Fix: Check the app store for updates.

  • Browser vs. App: Sometimes it's just the mobile app being finicky.

#Wise Account Locked Phone Issue – Unlocking Without Losing Your Balance

Locked out because of phone number issues? Don't keep trying to log in. Use Wise's email-based recovery, verify your identity with a passport or license, then update your contact method once you're back in.

Getting locked out sucks, but your money's usually safe.

  • Avoid Repeated Login Attempts: Multiple failed attempts trigger more security measures and temporary freezes.

  • Email-Based Recovery:

  • On the Wise login page, try Forgot password or Can't log in.

  • Log in with your registered email and password this might bypass SMS 2FA or at least start email recovery.

  • Identity Verification: Wise will likely ask you to verify your identity. Have ready:

  • A clear photo of government-issued ID (passport, driver's license).

  • A selfie or video verification.

  • Proof of address.

  • Update Contact Method: Once you're back in:

  • Go to Profile Settings.

  • Update your primary phone number to one you control.

  • Switch your primary 2FA to an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy). Less reliance on SMS and phone numbers.

  • Fund Safety: Wise doesn't freeze funds unless it suspects fraud. Your balance should be fine. Prolonged inactivity might trigger a temporary freeze, but that's fixed once you regain access.

Wise Signup Phone Number Conflict – One Number, Multiple Accounts, and the Rule

Wise policy states that there is one phone number and one account per person. Use the same number for a second account, and you'll immediately hit a signup phone number conflict error. No exceptions Wise requires unique contact info for every user.

This error is dead simple: Wise enforces one person, one account, one unique phone number.

  • Family or Shared Plans: Even on a family plan, if someone else used that number for their Wise account, you can't use it for yours. Wise sees the number, not who's holding the phone.

  • The Non-Negotiable Rule: Unlike some services, Wise won't let you have multiple accounts with the same phone number even with different emails or details. It's about KYC and fraud prevention.

  • Solutions:

  • Separate Number: Get a second line or eSIM from your carrier.

  • Temporary Virtual Number: Use a temporary number just for initial registration, then switch to a distinct personal number later.

  • No Cooldown: Sign-up phone number conflict errors are permanent per number. No reset will let you reuse it for a new account it's tied to the existing profile forever.

Is it legal to use a temporary phone number for Wise registration?

Yes, using a temporary phone number may be legal, but you should only use a number you’re authorized to use and must follow Wise’s terms and applicable local regulations. Why does my Wise verification code never arrive?

Common causes include carrier filtering, temporary rate limits, SMS delivery problems, or Wise not accepting the type of number you're using. Wait before requesting another code, check your phone and network settings, and make sure the number is from a supported region. If Wise does not accept virtual numbers, use a supported mobile number instead.

What’s the difference between a one-time virtual number and a rental number?

A one-time virtual number is intended for a single OTP and typically expires after the verification session. Can I transfer my Wise account to a new phone number later?

Yes, Wise may allow you to update the phone number associated with your account through your account settings. The exact process and verification requirements can vary, so follow the instructions shown in your Wise account when changing the number.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for on Wise?

Avoid using a temporary number for two-factor authentication if you may lose access to it later. You should also avoid using temporary numbers to bypass identity verification, security checks, or other Wise requirements.

Why does Wise say my phone number is already taken when I’ve never used Wise?

A recycled mobile number may have previously been associated with another person’s account. If you genuinely own the number, contact Wise support and follow its account-verification process. You can also use another number that you are authorized to use.

How do I stop getting phone number not accepted errors on Wise?

Enter the number in the correct international format, including the country code, and ensure Wise supports it.
