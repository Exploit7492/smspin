#Why Is My LINE OTP Not Received? The 5 Most Common Causes

LINE OTPs fail for a handful of predictable reasons: typos in the phone number, server-side delays, SMS blocklists, carrier filtering, or the number being previously registered. Nearly every case falls into one of these five buckets, so identifying the cause is the fastest route to a fix.

Here are the most common culprits:

  • Mistyped number or missing country code - the #1 cause globally, especially when autofill drops the + prefix.

  • LINE's own server backlog - code delivery can lag 5–10 minutes during peak hours.

  • Your phone's SMS blocklist or spam filter - short-code messages 5–6 digits get silently routed away.

  • Carrier-level spam filtering - many carriers throttle or block short-code SMS by default.

  • The number was recently used for LINE - cooldown or duplicate-prevention systems block immediate re-verification.

If any of these sound familiar, jump to the relevant fix below. Otherwise, start with the four quick checks next.

#Before You Troubleshoot: 4 Quick Checks That Solve 50% of Cases

Before diving into settings, do these four 30-second checks: verify the number is entered with the correct country code, hit the resend option once not repeatedly, switch from Wi-Fi to cellular data, and wait five full minutes. More than half of not received cases resolve with these basics alone.

  • Re-type the number manually instead of using autofill - autofill often omits the international prefix.

  • Use the resend option exactly once, then wait six minutes before retrying. Multiple requests can trigger a soft lock.

  • Toggle airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off, to force a fresh network connection.

  • Restart the LINE app completely swipe it away, don't just background it before requesting again.

#The Resend Code Button Isn't Working? Do This First

If LINE shows a resend button but tapping it does nothing, that's a client-side issue, not a server issue. The standard fix is to force-close the app, clear the app's cached data not your chat history, and re-initiate the verification flow.

On Android: Go to Settings → Apps → LINE → Storage → Clear Cache. Do not tap Clear Data unless you have a backup - that deletes local messages.

On iPhone: Go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → LINE → Offload App, then reinstall from the App Store. Your documents and data are preserved.

A few important notes:

  • If the resend button is grayed out, wait 60 seconds; LINE enforces a minimum interval between resends.

  • Never tap resend more than three times in 15 minutes - this can trigger an anti-spam lock for 24 hours.

  • After clearing the cache, you'll see the code text preview in your notifications if SMS delivery is working.

#The 3-Minute Rule - Why LINE OTP Delays Happen and How to Handle Them

LINE OTPs can take up to three minutes to arrive, and sometimes longer during peak hours or global outages. If a code hasn't arrived after 3–5 minutes, the original code is likely expired - wait exactly six minutes after the last request, then use the resend option once.

Here's what's happening behind the scenes:

  • OTPs are typically valid for 5–10 minutes, but LINE's counter starts at request time - not when you see the prompt.

  • Repeated resends invalidate previous codes; only the newest one works.

  • Global traffic spikes major holidays, new app launches can push delivery to 10+ minutes.

  • If the app shows a timer, wait until it finishes before requesting again.

Patience here is your best tool. Most delayed codes arrive within the 10-minute window if you stop tapping resend.

#Check Your SMS Blocklist and Spam Folder on Android & iPhone

Modern phones silently route short-code SMS 5–6 digit numbers into spam or blocklist folders without a notification. Check your messaging app's Spam, Blocked, or Filtered Messages folder - the code is often sitting there.

Samsung Messages: Tap the three dots → Settings → Block numbers and messages → Spam messages.

Google Messages: Open the app → tap your avatar → Spam & Blocked.

iPhone: Settings → Messages → Filter Unknown Senders, then check the Unknown Senders tab in your inbox.

Third-party SMS apps Truecaller, Pulse, Textra may have separate blocklists - check each one. If you find the LINE code in any of these folders, mark it as Not Spam and whitelist LINE's sender number to prevent future filtering.

#Fix LINE App Settings That Interfere with SMS Verification

LINE's in-app notification settings don't stop SMS delivery, but the app can auto-suppress the visual notification if Do Not Disturb or Chat Lock is active. Disable chat lock, check notification badges, and ensure LINE isn't in power-saving mode.

Walk through these settings:

  • Turn off Chat Lock and Hidden Chats - secret chats can suppress OTP notifications entirely.

  • Disable battery optimization for LINE - aggressive battery savers kill background SMS detection on both Android and iPhone.

  • Check LINE's Notification settings for Notifications from LINE and ensure SMS-related banners aren't muted.

  • If using LINE on a tablet or PC, the mobile app must remain in the foreground or background not force-closed to receive SMS verification prompts.

For official guidance on LINE's verification flow, the LINE Help Center documents the standard process and known issues.

#Network, Airplane Mode, and SIM Issues - When It's Not LINE's Fault

If your carrier is experiencing SMS short-code degradation, no LINE setting will help. Toggle off Wi-Fi calling which can delay SMS over Wi-Fi, remove and reinsert the SIM, and test receiving any SMS to isolate carrier issues.

Here's your checklist:

  • Disable Wi-Fi calling temporarily - SMS over Wi-Fi is notoriously laggy.

  • Physically remove and reinsert the SIM card, then reboot the phone.

  • Send a test SMS to your own number from another phone to confirm SMS works at all.

  • Roaming users: carriers often filter short-code SMS while roaming; enable data roaming and disable SMS-over-WiFi.

If your carrier blocks short-code messages entirely, no app-side fix will help. The FCC's SMS guidelines cover carrier obligations around SMS delivery, but the practical fix is often switching networks or using a virtual number.

#Clear LINE Cache and Reinstall Without Losing Chats

If the LINE app's internal state is corrupted, clearing cache or reinstalling can restore the verification flow. Back up your chat history to Google Drive or iCloud first - reinstalling does not delete your account, but local messages can vanish without a backup.

Android: Settings → Apps → LINE → Storage → Clear Cache never Clear Data unless you have a backup.

iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → LINE → Offload App preserves data, then reinstall from the App Store.

After reinstall, log in with your phone number and email, then restore the backup. If you use a password, ensure you know it - reinstalling may require re-authentication.

For detailed backup instructions, see Google's backup documentation and Apple's iPhone storage guide. Both outline how to preserve app data during reinstalls.

#FAQ

Is it legal to use a temporary number for LINE verification? 

Yes. Using a virtual number to verify an account you own is legal in most jurisdictions. SMSPin explicitly prohibits fraud, spam, or violating LINE's terms of service. Always use temporary numbers for legitimate account creation, privacy, or development testing.

Why do OTP codes fail even after resending? 

Repeated resend requests invalidate previous codes; only the latest code works. Additionally, if you've requested more than three resends, LINE may impose a temporary 24-hour lock. Wait 6 minutes after the latest request before trying again.

What's the difference between a one-time number and a rental number?

What should I NOT use temporary numbers for?

How long should I wait before giving up on a LINE OTP? 

Wait at least 10 minutes after your LAST resend request. If the code hasn't arrived by then, check the spam folder Fix 4, toggle airplane mode Fix 6, and only then consider a temporary number. Avoid requesting more than 3 resends within 15 minutes.

Can I receive a LINE OTP on a tablet or PC? 

LINE verification is SMS-based; the OTP goes to the phone number, not the device. If you're using LINE on a tablet, the SMS still lands on your mobile phone's SIM or your virtual number's dashboard. You'll need to enter the code manually on the tablet.

Does clearing LINE cache delete my chats? 

Clearing cache as opposed to clear data does not delete chat history. On Android, Clear Cache only removes temporary files. On iPhone, Offload App deletes the app but preserves documents and data, including chat history, until you reinstall.
