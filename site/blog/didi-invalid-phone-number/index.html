#What Does the DiDi Invalid Phone Number Error Actually Mean?

The short version: DiDi refuses to accept your number during signup or profile changes, even though the number is real and active. It's not a formatting problem. The platform runs checks on the carrier type, the number's history, and whether it's appeared on any spam or abuse watchlists. Think of it as a targeted block tied to the number's reputation, not a technical hiccup with your SIM card.

You might see prepaid or VoIP numbers get rejected more often, but the deeper issue is usually history. If your number was previously connected to spam or fraudulent activity even through no fault of your own it carries a reputation score that DiDi reads. So when we say new number, we really mean a number with a clean record, not just a different SIM. You can't type the same digits again and expect different results.

#Why Your Number Gets Rejected and Why Replacing Fixes the Biggest

So here's the thing just changing your number often fails if your account is still linked to the same IP or device that led to the block. You need to change the number and shake up how you access the app. The number is step one because it's central to your account identity, but DiDi also checks your device IMEI and IP address.

Sometimes the issue goes beyond the phone number itself. A platform may assess number reputation, IP address, and device signals, so a previously flagged number can cause legitimate verification to fail. Carrier or account restrictions may also affect SMS verification, even when the SIM itself is valid.

The background mechanics matter here. OWASP's guidelines on transport security show that OTPs are vulnerable if handled carelessly, which is why platforms like DiDi apply strict controls to the line number itself not just the confirmation code. The FCC has also documented how spam often traces back to internet-based numbers, which is why a dedicated, clean number carries so much weight. You're essentially reviewing your digital phone passport not just checking for typos.

#How to Fix the DiDi Error With a New Number

Stick to this order, or you'll keep repeating the same failure.

#Secure a New, Clean Number

Don't bother recycling an old number. Get a new number that can receive SMS texts. Two routes:

  • Paid Secure Option: A temporary number from an SMS service provider that cycles through fresh, usable numbers.

  • Free Option: Apps or a cheap prepaid SIM you pick up at a store. Heads-up: DiDi frequently flags these, so expect a higher failure rate.

#Prepare Your Device

Don't try the fix on your old phone. If you can't borrow another, put your new SIM into a different device. This resets the device fingerprint DiDi wants to forget.

  • Log out of DiDi completely.

  • Clear the app cache or uninstall and reinstall it.

  • Switch off your VPN or any IP proxy services.

  • Connect to a completely different home/mobile network than the one tied to your previous blocked attempts.

#Start the Number Change

Open DiDi and head to Profile → Account → Phone Number. Enter your new number details. The OTP will arrive via SMS.

#Verify and Wait

  • Type in the OTP.

  • Important: Wait 30 minutes before you log out or do anything else. This gives the server time to settle the change.

If you're stuck on verification, your number isn't passing the check. Go back to Step 1 and identify whether the number came from a paid service or a fresh SIM. If it still fails, jump to the troubleshooting section below.

#DiDi Driver Invalid Phone Number Error: Sign-Up and Fleet Account Fixes

Drivers hit this error more frequently if they've signed up using a batch of rented numbers that DiDi has already marked. If you submitted your license or eligibility documentation under the old phone number, you'll need to update the number and then complete a fresh background check. For driver onboarding, a stable prepaid number or a landline-based number that remains active for at least 7 days is your best bet. Anything shorter, and your account may be restricted.

Driver signup often involves more checks than rider verification, including OTP confirmation and additional account details. Free numbers without a SIM may be less suitable if DiDi requires a supported mobile number, while a rented number may be an option where permitted. Using the same number across personal and driver accounts can also cause verification issues.

#DiDi Rider Invalid Error: Replacing a Flagged Number Before Heading Out

For most riders, this error pops up when you order a ride after changing carriers, or after the system issues a security trip. Good news you can edit your passenger account directly in the app, so a new number resolves most sign-up and login mismatches. If it doesn't fix it, the issue may be tied to bundle features or old session data the app is still tracking.

Your practical fix: update the number in Profile before making a ride request to avoid triggering a ride refusal. If the code fails, don't reinstall your old number. Instead, contact DiDi support and ask them to purge the old number from their database. One thing to remember DiDi blocks VoIP numbers like Google Voice and TextNow, so use a standard, paid SMS number.

#Troubleshooting: Why Your New Number Might Still Fail DiDi Verification

Fresh doesn't always equal passable. Here's what's likely happening:

#The Unclean Device Problem

If you're using the same app/data you had before, your new number is being associated with old bad data.

The fix:

  • Do not use a VPN. DiDi is gatekeeping your IP to match your local region or your number's region.

  • Put your new SIM into a different phone than the original that triggered the error. If that's not possible, use the web version on a different browser.

#The Gray Reputation Number

Some numbers aren't blocked but are flagged as suspicious because they were recently activated.

How to handle:

  • Do NOT spam OTP requests. Max 3 tries.

  • Responsible way: wait 12 hours before retrying.

#The Region Issue

If your new number is outside the same country as your documents, you'll get a risky error.

The fix:

  • You need a number from the same region as your passport and yes, using a VPN will trigger a block even with the correct region.

#How to Avoid Flagging Your New Number

Every new number online real or virtual starts from a cold start reputation. What screws it up is linking it to spam-app histories right away. If you ruin that step, no batch of new numbers will help you're chasing the same block.

The golden rule of Safe OTP:

  • Never use your new DiDi number to sign up for a bunch of other apps at the same time.

  • Don't put it in ten different Telegram groups within a day.

Checklist before you enter a new OTP:

  • Designate a primary purpose DiDi.

  • Use it only from a clean IP.

  • Don't request a resend you'd look like a bot.

  • Link the number to an email address that builds identity linkage that platforms trust.

Realists wait 12–24 hours between failed attempts, or the number gets a permanent lifetime ban. That's how you build trust in the system's eyes.

#Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal to use a temporary phone number for DiDi verification?

Using a temporary number for legitimate account verification can be acceptable, provided it complies with DiDi's terms and applicable local laws. Don't use temporary numbers for fraud, fake bonuses, identity deception, ban evasion, or other prohibited activities.

Why does DiDi show invalid phone number when my number is correct?

A correctly formatted number can still be rejected if DiDi doesn't support the number type, carrier, region, or verification history. Check the country code and formatting first. If everything is correct, try another supported mobile number or contact DiDi support rather than repeatedly switching numbers to bypass restrictions.

How long does a rental number from SMSPin work for DiDi?

The available duration depends on the specific SMSPin number and rental option you select. A one-time activation is intended for a single verification, while longer rentals keep the number available for an extended period. Check the current rental duration before purchasing.

Should I use a temporary number for my DiDi driver account?

Driver onboarding may involve additional identity and eligibility checks beyond phone verification. Don't use a temporary number as a substitute for required identity information or documentation. If DiDi requires a stable, long-term phone number for a driver account, use a number that you can reliably access.

Why is my new number still showing an invalid error after purchase?

The number itself may not be supported, or DiDi may have temporarily restricted verification attempts based on account, device, network, or other security signals. Avoid trying to circumvent those controls by repeatedly changing devices, IP addresses, or numbers. Check the number format and contact DiDi support if the error persists.

What should NOT be linked with a new temporary number?

Avoid using temporary numbers for online banking, financial services, government accounts, or other high-stakes services that require dependable long-term account recovery. Don't use them for referral abuse, fraudulent transactions, or schemes intended to generate illegitimate rewards.

I received the code but failed to enter it in time. What's next?

Request a new verification code after the previous one has expired, following DiDi's displayed instructions. Make sure you're entering the latest code and avoid repeatedly requesting new codes in a short period, as this can trigger temporary rate limits. If verification continues to fail, use DiDi's official support process.
