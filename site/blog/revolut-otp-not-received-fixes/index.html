#Why Revolut OTPs Fail: The 5 Real Reasons Your Code Isn't Arriving

Revolut OTPs rarely fail because of your account itself. It's almost always a delivery problem linked to SMS filtering, an inactive SIM slot, blocked app notifications, an outdated OS, or the phone's default SMS app hiding the code in a spam folder. Fix the delivery path, and you fix the code.

The five root causes:

  • SMS filtering: Many Android phones and carriers silently sort OTPs into a Spam or Blocked folder without any notification.

  • Carrier or network issues: Roaming, VoLTE settings, or constant signal handoffs can delay SMS delivery by minutes.

  • Push vs. SMS conflict: Revolut prefers push notifications. When the app's notification settings clash with the OS, the code arrives but never appears on your screen.

  • Dormant SIM or recent swap: If you've changed devices or carriers, the network might not validate the SMS profile properly.

  • Provider throttling: In high-traffic regions like India or Brazil, operator-level SMS delay is common. The code lands - just late.

#First 5-Minute Fixes: Check Phone Settings Before You Panic

These fixes take about five minutes total and will resolve a surprisingly high number of cases. Start here, before you do anything more drastic.

  • Check the spam folders first: Open your default SMS app and check Spam, Blocked, or Archive. Your Revolut code may already be there.

  • Toggle Airplane Mode: Turn it on briefly, then switch it off to refresh the cellular connection.

  • Restart your phone: A proper reboot can clear temporary network or messaging issues.

  • Confirm the phone number on your account: Make sure the number shown in your Revolut account matches the SIM currently in your phone. If needed, free numbers may be another option where supported.

#Android-Specific Fixes: Why Revolut SMS Codes Fail on Samsung, Pixel & More

The most common culprit on Android is battery optimization. Revolut's aggressive battery management kills background processes, including the SMS delivery service. The second biggest issue is the spam filter baked into the default messaging app.

  • Disable battery optimization for Revolut and your SMS app. Go to Settings > Apps, find Revolut, then Battery > Unrestricted. Do the same for your messaging app. This stops Android from killing the processes that deliver verification codes.

  • Turn off SMS spam protection. Samsung's Messages app has a toggle that filters certain messages. Uncheck it.

  • Remove third-party SMS filters temporarily. Apps like Truecaller or Google Call Screen can intercept verification codes. Uninstall them temporarily and test again.

  • Check Digital Wellbeing and Focus mode. Those features can silence SMS alerts entirely - though the code will still appear inside the message thread when you open the app.

#iPhone-Specific Fixes: Why iMessage and Other iOS Settings Block Your Revolut Code

The most common iOS issue is a conflict with SMS forwarding. iMessage routes your SMS to a Mac or iPad, but if those devices are offline, the code never surfaces. Follow these checks in order:

  • Disable Text Message Forwarding. Open Settings > Messages > Text Message Forwarding and turn it off. Or make sure every listed device is actually powered on and up to date. This single toggle has fixed more delayed OTPs than anything else on iOS.

  • Check Send & Receive. Under Settings > Messages > Send & Receive, check that your Apple ID and phone number are synced. If something has changed recently, sign out and back in.

  • Turn off Filter Unknown Senders. When this is on, messages from unknown numbers like Revolut's short codes get sorted into a separate list and won't trigger a notification. Settings > Messages > Filter Unknown Senders = OFF.

  • Try disabling WiFi Calling. Settings > Phone > Wi-Fi Calling. If your SMS is stuck, turning this off temporarily can force the code through the cellular network instead, which often delivers faster.

#The App Itself: How to Reset Revolut Push Notifications and Request a New Code

Even with a fully functional SIM, the Revolut app can struggle to display incoming push notifications. Here's how to fix that.

Force-stop the app and clear its cache. Then open Settings > Security > Push Notifications inside the Revolut app and toggle them off and on. If that doesn't work, log out and log back in to re-sync the notification channel.

When you're back on the OTP screen, tap I haven't received a code to request a new one. Important: Wait 60 seconds before resending, because Revolut temporarily blocks new codes if you request them too aggressively.

#SIM, Carrier, and Network Settings: Hidden Issues Blocking Your OTP

Sometimes the phone, WiFi, and app are all fine - the problem is your carrier or SIM profile. This one's harder to diagnose but luckily fairly straightforward.

  • Confirm the SIM is active. Put the SIM in an old phone and send a test SMS to a friend. If that fails, call your carrier. Your carrier may have blocked short codes on your line.

  • Disable Wi-Fi Calling. Some carriers, particularly in the UK, have SMS delays when Wi-Fi Calling remains enabled. Toggle it off temporarily and test again.

  • Roaming is a known culprit. If you're traveling, your SIM needs Data Roaming enabled to deliver SMS. If you only have voice roaming active, SMS OTPs can get stuck indefinitely.

  • eSIM swaps can break the SMS path. If the OTP worked before but fails after an eSIM swap, contact your carrier for a new profile. The old profile lingers and causes rejection at the network level.

If your carrier can't be fixed in minutes, the fastest workaround is a virtual number.

#Revolut OTP Not Received After Signup? The Registration and Dormant Account Fix

Missing a code right after account creation is often part of Revolut's risk filter system. New accounts that trip a security check may see OTP suppression for a short window.

  • Wait 5-10 minutes and try again, or log out and back in. If the flag is purely temporary, this clears it.

  • Switch delivery mode. On the login screen, look for Resend via voice call and use that instead of SMS. The automated voice reads the code out, usually within 30 seconds.

  • Resend cadence matters. Revolut temporarily flags accounts that request too many OTPs too quickly. Watch between attempts.

  • Dormant: keep an eye out. If your number worked before but stops delivering after weeks of inactivity, Revolut may have flagged the account as low-trust. Contact support in that case or use the voice call route instead.

#Contacting Revolut Support: The Fastest Way to Get a Human and Fix OTP Issues

Revolut's support has a few tiers, so here's the most efficient channel for each issue:

  • Email tickets: If chat isn't available, email Revolut's support address with your account details - they'll usually respond within a few hours.
