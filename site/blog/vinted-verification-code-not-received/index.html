#Why Your Vinted Code Is Not Sending: 5 Most Common Culprits

If your Vinted verification code is not sending, the fault is almost certainly on your end  not Vinted's servers. Carriers, phone settings, and tiny formatting mistakes cause most of these issues. Before you panic and start spamming the resend button, run through these five checks. You'll probably find the fix in under a minute.

  • Carrier spam filtering: Some mobile carriers don't like shortcode SMS from marketplaces. They quietly block them. Check your carrier's block list or call their support.

  • Do Not Disturb (DND) settings on Android: If DND is active, incoming verification texts can get silently murdered. Flip that setting off before requesting a fresh code.

  • Full SMS inbox: Believe it or not, many phones silently reject new messages when the inbox is crammed full. Delete some old texts and try again.

  • Unsaved or incorrect number format: Vinted expects the full international prefix such as +44 for the UK. Even a missing + can completely kill delivery.

  • Temporary network glitch: Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or toggle airplane mode. That little reset often reconnects you to Vinted's SMS gateway properly.

#Vinted SMS Not Arriving? Check Your Phone Settings First

When your Vinted SMS messages aren't arriving, your phone is often the silent saboteur. Two settings in particular  SMS blocking and message filtering  can catch verification codes before they ever reach your notification bar. Here's exactly where to look on both iOS and Android.

  • iOS Message Filtering: Head to Settings > Messages > Unknown & Spam. Make sure no filter app is blocking Vinted's sender number. Sometimes third-party spam apps get a little too aggressive.

  • Android SMS Blocking: Open your SMS app, go to Settings > Blocked numbers or Spam protection. Remove any entries that might accidentally match Vinted's shortcodes.

  • Dual SIM issues: If you're running a secondary SIM just for data, Vinted might send the code to the wrong line. Make sure the SIM associated with the number you entered is active.

  • Third-party SMS apps: Apps like Textra or Pulse sometimes apply their own spam rules. Switch to the default Messages app temporarily to see if that fixes delivery.

#Why Am I Not Getting My Vinted Code on WhatsApp or VoIP Numbers?

Vinted rarely sends verification codes to VoIP numbers or WhatsApp-based lines. Why? Those numbers don't have a real SIM carrier attachment. Vinted specifically looks for mobile numbers tied to an active cellular network. So, did you try a Google Voice, TextNow, or Skype number? That's exactly why your Vinted confirmation code is missing.

  • VoIP detection: Vinted uses carrier lookup databases. Numbers from VoIP providers get flagged as non-physical and blocked from verification. Simple as that.

  • WhatsApp numbers don't work: Even if you use WhatsApp on that number, Vinted requires a direct mobile connection via traditional SMS. WhatsApp is not a carrier.

  • Landline fails the test: A fixed-line number can't receive SMS at all. Vinted's system will error out silently  no explanation given.

  • Prepaid vs postpaid: In some regions, prepaid numbers from certain MVNOs may also get rejected if they're flagged as temporary or disposable.

#Troubleshoot Vinted SMS That Isn't Arriving

When your Vinted SMS won't show up, work through this five-step troubleshooting drill  no special tools required. From checking your spam folder to switching networks, each step takes under a minute. This is the fastest way to figure out whether the fault is on your phone, your carrier, or Vinted.

  • Step 1  Spam check: Open your SMS app's Spam or Blocked folder. Many Android phones auto-route shortcodes there without telling you.

  • Step 2  Network reset: Toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds, then turn it off and request a new code.

  • Step 3  SIM test: Remove the SIM and reinsert it. This forces a fresh network registration that can unstick pending messages.

  • Step 4  Try a different device: If possible, pop the SIM into another phone and request the code again. This eliminates device-specific filtering.

  • Step 5  Wait and retry: Vinted's SMS gateway may have a temporary delay. Wait 15 minutes, then request a fresh code  don't use the previous resend button.
