You've typed your number in carefully checked every digit and Mercari still hits you with "invalid phone number." Frustrating doesn't quite cover it, does it?

Here's the thing you need to know before you rage-quit: your number probably isn't "broken." The problem is how Mercari's verification system classifies it. This error is Mercari's way of saying your number doesn't fit its risk profile not that it doesn't exist.

This guide breaks down exactly why Mercari rejects certain numbers and walks you through practical, step-by-step fixes. Whether you're on iPhone or Android, dealing with carrier headaches, or wondering if a virtual number could save you, we've got you covered.

#What "Invalid Phone Number" Actually Means on Mercari

When Mercari says your phone number is invalid, it doesn't mean the number doesn't exist. It means Mercari's verification system has flagged the number as ineligible for receiving SMS verification codes usually because it's a VoIP service, a landline, a prepaid carrier flagged by their system, or the country code doesn't match your account region.

Think of it this way: your carrier thinks your number is perfectly fine. Mercari's risk engine disagrees. These are two different standards, and understanding that distinction is the first step to fixing the error without wasting time retyping the same number.

Mercari relies on third-party phone verification services that classify numbers as postpaid mobile, prepaid mobile, VoIP, toll-free, or landline. Only some categories pass the gate. It's an anti-fraud measure Mercari wants to confirm a human with a real SIM card is behind the account, not a bot using disposable numbers.

If you're using a number from a country where you don't have a Mercari account, the system will auto-reject it. This same logic applies across many platforms that rely on similar SMS verification services to confirm user identity.

#The 6 Real Reasons Mercari Rejects Your Number

Mercari rejects phone numbers for six main reasons: the number is a VoIP/virtual number, the number is associated with previous fraud attempts, your country code doesn't match your account region, the number is a landline, you're using a prepaid carrier that Mercari's provider blocks, or the number was recently ported. Each cause has a different fix, so don't just re-enter the same digits first identify which category you fall into.

  • VoIP/virtual: Google Voice, TextNow, and similar services are almost always blocked because they're free and easily abused.

  • Country mismatch: A US-based Mercari account requires a US number. Using a UK or India number will trigger the error instantly.

  • Prepaid blocks: Some prepaid carriers especially MVNOs share number pools with spam operations, so Mercari's provider flags them.

  • Landline: Mercari needs SMS delivery; landlines can't receive traditional texts, and even if they can via carrier features, they're not classified as mobile.

  • Recently ported numbers: Ported numbers take 24–72 hours to propagate through carrier databases; during that window, verification may fail.

  • Risk engine flags: If your IP address, device, or number was seen in a prior fraud cluster, Mercari blocks it preemptively.

The takeaway? This isn't random. It's a deterministic system with specific inputs. Once you understand those inputs, you can work around them.

#Why Mercari Blocks VoIP, Virtual, and Prepaid Numbers And Why It's Not Personal

Mercari blocks VoIP and virtual numbers because they're free, easy to program, and heavily used in smishing, account farming, and marketplace fraud. Prepaid numbers get flagged more often because they require no identity verification to acquire, making them attractive to scammers. It's not a judgment on you it's a risk score dictated by the verification provider Mercari uses.

VoIP numbers like Google Voice or Skype route calls over the internet rather than physical SIM infrastructure; they're trivially easy to replace if banned. Prepaid numbers in certain regions like India and Brazil are frequently recycled through multiple owners, so the previous owner's reputation may taint your number.

Mercari's verification provider maintains a "phone reputation score" based on previous fraud reports associated with that number or number range. If you absolutely need a virtual number, your only realistic option is a paid, carrier-grade virtual number service that rents real SIM infrastructure rather than VoIP freebies. The same number that fails on Mercari may work perfectly on other apps each platform has different provider and risk thresholds.

#Check Your Number Format: The Simplest Fix You're Skipping

Before you blame your carrier, verify you're entering the number in the exact international format Mercari expects: country code + plus full national number, with no leading zeros, spaces, or dashes. Enter a US number as +1XXXXXXXXXX, a UK number as +44XXXXXXXXXX, and an Indian number as +91XXXXXXXXXX.

Many users fail here because their phone autofill inserts spaces or the "0" used in domestic dialing. It's a silent killer of verification attempts.

  • Remove any spaces, hyphens, or parentheses; Mercari's form is strict and won't auto-strip them.

  • If you're in a country that uses national trunk prefixes like "0" in the UK, you must drop that zero when using the +44 format.

  • Test the number by copying it into a simple "send SMS to this number" prompt if it formats correctly elsewhere, it's likely fine.

  • Double-check your region code: your account's country and your phone number's country should match exactly.

  • If you're on a dual-SIM phone, make sure you're entering the number of the SIM you intend to receive the code on, not the primary display SIM.

This formatting issue is common enough that telecom industry bodies like the GSMA publish extensive documentation on proper international number formatting. Getting this right removes the most common hurdle.

#How to Fix Mercari Invalid Phone Number Error on iPhone iOS

On iOS, start by turning off iMessage and FaceTime for 30 minutes, since Apple's registration system can interfere with SMS routing, then re-enter your number in international format. If that fails, check your carrier's SMS delivery settings, temporarily disable Wi-Fi calling, and make sure your iOS software is up to date.

The most common iOS-specific culprit? iMessage hijacking your SMS. When iMessage intercepts a message meant for your carrier's SMS pipeline, the verification code never arrives. You're left waiting for a text that's been silently swallowed.

  • Navigate to Settings > Messages > Send & Receive, sign out of your Apple ID, then back in this refreshes your SMS registration.

  • Toggle off Wi-Fi Calling: Settings > Phone > Wi-Fi Calling. VoIP-over-Wi-Fi can route texts through a server that Mercari flags.

  • Update iOS to the latest version; older builds have known SMS delivery bugs. Apple's own troubleshooting guide for iMessage and SMS outlines these steps in more detail.

  • Contact your carrier and ask if your line has "SMS blocking" enabled, which many carriers add by default for anti-spam.

  • Try sending a test SMS to your own number from a friend's phone if you don't receive it, the issue is carrier-side, not Mercari-side.

#How to Fix Mercari Invalid Phone Number Error on Android

On Android, the fix most often involves disabling RCS Rich Communication Services, aka Chat features in your Messages app, because RCS routes SMS through Google's server and can interfere with short-code verification. You should also check if your number is set to "private" in your carrier settings, and confirm your default messaging app is actually the one receiving SMS.

Here's the thing most Android users don't realize: if you use a third-party SMS app, short-code messages may not arrive at all. The app isn't built to handle them, and you're left wondering why Mercari never sent the code.

  • Disable RCS: Open Messages > Settings > RCS Chats > Turn off. Wait 10 minutes, then retry.

  • Set your number to "public" in your carrier's caller ID settings private numbers often fail verification.

  • Ensure your SIM is inserted correctly and your phone shows full network bars; no signal means no SMS.

  • In Android settings, go to Apps > Messages > Storage > Clear Cache not Clear Data, then retry.

  • If you're using a dual-SIM phone, disable the inactive SIM some manufacturers route SMS through the wrong slot.

#What to Do If Your Carrier is the Problem Port-Out, Postpaid, or Coverage

If your number passes format checks but Mercari still rejects it, the problem may be carrier-level. Recently ported numbers take up to 72 hours to update across SMS databases; postpaid numbers on smaller regional carriers sometimes have poor short-code routing; and rural coverage gaps can delay SMS indefinitely.

The solution? Wait out the port, request a carrier number sync, or use a different number entirely. It's not glamorous, but it's realistic.

  • If you ported your number in the last 3 days, wait 72 hours before retrying the SMS routing database may not recognize you yet.

  • Call your carrier and ask them to "re-sync SMS routing" or "refresh the HLR" Home Location Register for your number.

  • Some smaller MVNOs have known sporadic issues with short-code SMS delivery; check their status pages.

  • If you're in a remote area, move to a location with better signal SMS delivery times are often length-based and coverage-dependent.

  • If your carrier can't resolve it, your only reliable option is to use a different phone number for verification.

This is also a personal security issue. The FCC's guidance on SIM swapping and port-out fraud explains why carriers are so protective of number changes and why Mercari is too.

#Mercari Phone Verification Failed? Here's the Step-by-Step Reset

If verification fails after you enter a valid number, do a full reset: log out of Mercari, clear the app cache, verify your number on Mercari's website instead of in the app, then log back in. This bypasses app-specific bugs and resets the verification session.

If the number itself was rejected, request a new verification code rather than tapping "resend" repeatedly repeated requests can trigger a temporary cooldown. Patience here actually saves you time.

  • Log out of Mercari completely, not just close the app.

  • Go to your phone's app settings and clear Mercari's cache and storage.

  • Open Mercari in a desktop/mobile browser and attempt verification there this uses a different verification flow.

  • If the browser fails too, wait 15 minutes between attempts Mercari sets a cooldown on failed code requests.

  • Step 5: If you still can't verify, check Mercari's official help center for a service outage affecting SMS verification.

#Mercari Account Security: Why Verification Matters Beyond the Error

Mercari's phone verification isn't just a nuisance it's the gatekeeper between your account and scammers who want to list fake products, launder money, or hijack your identity. When you bypass verification with a fraudulent number, you don't just risk losing the account; you risk being implicated in illegal activity.

The "invalid number" error is Mercari's way of saying, "We can't confirm you're real, so we won't let you operate." Annoying? Sure. But it's also protecting the marketplace you're trying to use.

  • Mercari's seller verification system requires a confirmed phone number to activate listing privileges for high-value items.

  • Scammers use stolen credit cards to buy items, then sell them quickly via accounts with fake numbers Mercari's phone check is the primary barrier to this.

  • If your number fails verification and you use a number that's already flagged, your account may receive a permanent ban rather than just an error.

  • A verified phone number also protects you as a buyer it makes account recovery easier if you're hacked.

  • Keep your real number private where possible; using a temporary number for the verification step doesn't compromise your account security, as long as you keep the account active and legitimate.

Robust identity verification is well documented. NIST's digital identity verification guidelines outline why platforms like Mercari implement these checks it's about protecting the entire ecosystem, not just individual accounts.

#Essential Troubleshooting Checklist: Mercari Phone Number Not Accepted

Here's the full diagnostic checklist in order: 1 Verify international format, 2 Confirm country code matches account region, 3 Disable RCS Android or iMessage iPhone, 4 Wait 72 hours if recently ported, 5 Test SMS reception from another phone, 6 Clear app cache and retry via browser, 7 Wait 15 minutes between attempts, 8 If all fails, use an alternate verified number.

Follow these steps sequentially don't skip ahead. Each one eliminates a potential cause, and jumping around just wastes time.

  • Check format: +[country code][number], no spaces or leading zeros.

  • Check region: Your Mercari account country and phone number country must match exactly.

  • Disable RCS/iMessage: These services can hijack SMS delivery and cause verification codes to fail silently.

  • Check porting: If you moved numbers recently, wait 3 full days.

  • Test manually: Send a regular SMS to your number from a friend's phone, and make sure it arrives.

  • Clear and retry: Clear app cache, log out, and try verification via browser.

  • Alternate number: If everything fails after 2 hours of attempts, your number is likely blocked at the verification-provider level use a different number.

#FAQ

Is it legal to use a temporary or virtual number for Mercari verification?

Yes, using a virtual number to receive an OTP is legal in most countries, as long as you're not using it to evade a ban, commit fraud, or violate Mercari's terms of service. However, Mercari's terms may prohibit using numbers you don't own permanently, so use temporary numbers only for testing or when your real number is genuinely blocked. Always follow Mercari's user agreement and local regulations.

Why does Mercari say my phone number is invalid when it works fine for calls and regular texts?

Because Mercari and its verification provider don't just check if the number exists they check if it's a legitimate postpaid mobile number. If you're using a VoIP, prepaid from a flagged MVNO, or a number that's been the subject of prior fraud reports, the system blocks it regardless of its real-world functionality. Your number works fine for calls, but it fails Mercari's specific risk threshold.

Can I use a temporary number to receive Mercari's verification code and then switch back to my real number?

Technically, yes, but Mercari may re-verify your number later especially before selling high-value items or withdrawing funds. If you can't reverify with the same number, Mercari may limit your account. If you're testing the flow, a temporary number works fine. If you plan to use Mercari long-term, use your real postpaid number.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for on Mercari?

Do not use a temporary number for a Mercari account you intend to use long-term for selling, receiving payments, or maintaining a buyer reputation score. Do not use it to create multiple accounts to bypass Mercari's selling limits or avoid seller fees. And absolutely never use a temporary number to commit fraud Mercari will ban the account and may report the activity to authorities.

Why did my code never arrive when Mercari said verification was successful?

This usually happens for one of three reasons: your carrier is blocking short-code SMS messages contact them to lift the block, your phone's SMS app isn't set as the default, or the code was delivered to a secondary SIM slot you weren't monitoring. Check all messaging apps on your device, wait 5 minutes, and request a new code. If you used a temporary number service and the code didn't arrive, use a service with an automatic refund policy so you're not charged.

How many times can I try re-entering my phone number on Mercari before I get locked out?

Mercari typically allows 3–5 attempts before imposing a temporary 24-hour cooldown on verification. Re-attempting repeatedly within a short window won't help you'll extend the lockout. If your first 2 attempts fail, stop, diagnose the cause using the troubleshooting checklist above, and then retry after a 15-minute wait. If you exceed the max attempts, wait 24 hours before trying again.

Does Mercari accept international phone numbers for accounts created in the US?

No the country code on your phone number must match the country associated with your Mercari account. If you create a US account and try to verify with a UK or India number, Mercari will instantly flag it as invalid. Set up your Mercari account in the country where you're physically located and use a matching phone number.
