#Why Medium SMS Not Delivered? The Real Reasons Behind the Silence

Medium uses SMS verification when you sign up with a phone number instead of an email, or when you enable two-factor authentication. When the text never shows up, the problem usually comes down to one of three things: your carrier quietly filters shortcode messages, the number you entered was recycled from someone else who got it flagged, or Medium's delivery system is just slow. Figuring out which one applies to you takes about a minute, and most of the time, you can fix it without any help.

  • Shortcode blocking happens often on prepaid plans and business lines where carriers filter mass-market SMS more aggressively to protect users from spam.

  • Number recycling means the previous owner might have triggered spam flags, causing Medium to block that number permanently.

  • International numbers can take longer because they route across different telecommunications networks.

  • A simple typo in your country code is more common than you'd think; double-check the format before troubleshooting anything else.

#Medium Verification Code Not Received: Immediate Checks to Run First

Before you start blaming Medium, run through this quick five-point audit. Verify the phone number you entered matches your device's SIM exactly, confirm you have a stable cellular signal, not just Wi-Fi, and check your SMS app's blocked messages folder for any auto-filtered texts. Also, make sure your phone's date and time settings are set to automatic, because encryption mismatches can cause silent drops. Finally, try a fresh request after a full minute has passed, since spamming the resend button often resets Medium's delivery timer.

  • Check your blocked numbers list; some Android and iOS versions auto-hide shortcode messages.

  • Toggle airplane mode on and off to force your device to re-register with the carrier tower.

  • Ensure your SIM is not in roaming-only mode, which sometimes deprioritizes SMS delivery.

  • Write down the exact time you requested the code; Medium's codes typically expire within 10 minutes.

  • If you have a dual-SIM phone, confirm the number you registered is the active line for SMS.

#How to Fix Medium SMS Not Delivered: Resend, Switch, and Wait Tactics

The fastest fix is to request a new code, but do it strategically. Wait at least 60 seconds after the first request fails before hitting resend; rapid retries can flag your session as spam. If the second attempt fails, log out of the Medium app and try the verification on a different device or browser. Still nothing after the third try? Switch your signup method to email temporarily, then add your phone number later from within account settings; this often bypasses the broken SMS verification flow entirely.

  • Use Medium's Send a new code link found directly under the OTP input field.

  • Try requesting the code from a desktop browser instead of the mobile app.

  • Ask a friend to send a regular SMS to your number to confirm your device can receive texts at all.

  • If your phone barely has reception, move to a different room or go near a window before retrying.

  • Medium's SMS provider might be down globally; check an outage tracker to confirm it's not on their end.

#Medium OTP Not Arriving? Carrier Filtering and Number Recycling Explained

Carrier-side filtering is the silent killer of OTPs. Many network operators, especially in the US and UK, automatically silence messages from unknown shortcodes if they trigger spam heuristics, and they rarely notify the recipient. Number recycling is the second culprit: if your number previously belonged to someone who spammed Medium, the platform may have permanently flagged that number for SMS verification. Your only reliable workaround is to use a fresh, virtual phone number that has never been associated with Medium.

  • Prepaid carriers and MVNOs filter more aggressively than postpaid plans.

  • Test with a temporary virtual number to confirm whether the issue is your physical SIM or Medium's system.

  • If you're on a corporate or VoIP plan, these lines often cannot receive standard shortcode messages.

  • Number recycling is more common with newer area codes or numbers you ported from another provider.

  • If you're in the US, a US temporary number can bypass local filtering issues.

#Medium Verification Text Message Issue on Mobile vs. Desktop

Medium handles SMS verification differently depending on where you start. On the mobile app, the flow is often faster because it pre-fills your country code, but it's more prone to carrier delays. On desktop, the flow is more standard, but browser-based pop-up blockers can interfere with the redirect after verification. If the code works on one device but not the other, the issue isn't Medium; it's how that device handles SMS or session tokens.

  • Clear your browser cache or app cache before retrying to eliminate stale session data.

  • Use a private or incognito window on desktop to rule out extension interference, such as ad blockers.

  • On mobile, force-close the Medium app completely, then reopen it rather than just switching tabs.

  • Check if you're using a VPN: some VPN exit nodes are flagged, causing Medium to drop SMS requests silently.

  • If you use an iOS device, ensure SMS is enabled for the Medium app in your settings.

#Medium SMS Not Delivered Business Verification: Enterprise Account Setup

Business verification often fails because companies try to use a main office landline, a toll-free number, or an auto-attendant line that can't receive Direct-to-Consumer SMS. Medium expects a direct, SMS-capable mobile number that can receive a one-time passcode. For enterprise signups, bypass any IVR or switchboard layer and use a dedicated mobile number or a reputable virtual number service that isn't behind a corporate firewall.

  • Never use a VoIP number from your corporate PBX; Medium's SMS gateway will likely reject it outright.

  • Create a separate business entity profile with a dedicated mobile line rather than your personal SIM.

  • If your company uses a global SIM with roaming, test it in the home country first to eliminate routing issues.

  • Document the verification failure timestamp and the exact error message for your IT team to review.

  • Use a second-number app that provides a clean, dedicated SIM profile for verification purposes only.

#Medium Verification Code for Team Accounts: Troubleshooting Bulk Invites

When adding multiple team members to a Medium publication or organization, SMS verification often fails for one user while succeeding for another because of the input lag between invites. Medium can throttle multiple SMS requests from the same IP or session, sending codes to earlier invitees but blocking later ones. The solution: space out invite requests by at least 5 minutes and have each new member verify from a different network, like their personal hotspot.

  • Send invites one at a time rather than using the bulk-add feature if SMS verification is required.

  • Ask team members to verify on their mobile network, not the office Wi-Fi, to avoid shared IP flags.

  • If one user's code arrives but another's doesn't, don't reuse the first; each code is unique.

  • Have an admin resend the invite from scratch rather than reactivating a pending invite.

  • For large teams, set a deadline and batch verification windows to avoid overlapping OTP requests.

#Medium SMS Delivery Business Support: When to Contact Medium and What to Say

If you've tried every device, network, and number combination and still get no OTP, it's time to escalate to Medium support. Go to Medium's Help Center and submit a ticket under the Account Access category, mentioning SMS verification failed in the subject line. Provide your account email, country code, full phone number (masked if you prefer), timezone, and the exact time you requested the code; this helps their team determine whether the block is carrier-side or platform-side.

  • Include your carrier name, because Medium can allow specific number ranges if they identify a provider issue.

  • Ask specifically for a shortcode delivery status check to see if the SMS was sent or bounced.

  • Mention if you've already tried a different number successfully, as this confirms your account isn't the issue.

  • If you're a business on a premium plan, reference your organization ID to route the ticket to their priority queue.

  • Wait 24–48 hours for a response; Medium's support team is slow but generally resolves verification issues on their end.

#Resolve Medium SMS Delivery Problem for Good: Preventative Settings and Best Practices

Once you finally receive the code, commit to preventing this headache from recurring. Add your phone number to your Medium account only after your email is verified, and enable two-factor authentication via an authenticator app instead of SMS as your primary method. If you must use SMS, update your profile to use a number that you know accepts shortcodes. If you need to verify multiple Medium accounts repeatedly, reserving a dedicated virtual number for all verification is the most sustainable long-term strategy. It keeps your personal SIM clean, avoids carrier throttling, and maintains a single point of contact for all OTP needs.

  • Switch your Medium 2FA method from SMS to an app like Google Authenticator to avoid future delivery issues. This aligns with OWASP best practices.

  • Test your verification number monthly by requesting a code and canceling the login.

  • Keep your contact info updated in Medium's Security settings to avoid having your number flagged as dormant.

  • For teams, standardize the onboarding workflow so every new member uses the same verified virtual number provider.

  • For ongoing access, rent a temporary number for a day to maintain a stable, verified line without touching your personal SIM.

#FAQ

Is it legal to use a temporary virtual number for Medium verification?

Yes, using a temporary virtual number to verify your own account is legal in most jurisdictions. However, it must be your own account, and you must follow Medium's terms of service. Using temp numbers to evade bans, spam, or create fraudulent accounts violates those terms. SMSPin is not affiliated with any app or website. Please follow each app's terms and local regulations.

Why does my code arrive on my friend's phone but not on mine?

The difference is usually your carrier, not your phone. Some carriers, especially budget MVNOs, silently block shortcode messages. Your friend's carrier likely forwards those codes without filtering. Try switching to a postpaid carrier or using a virtual number to bypass the filter.

Can I rent a number for a longer Medium verification process?

Yes, if you need a number for more than a one-time code, for instance, if you're setting up a business account and expect multiple OTPs, renting a number for a day or a week is a viable option. One-time numbers work for a single code, but rentals are better for ongoing team or multi-step verification.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for on Medium?

Never use a temporary number to bypass a banned account, create duplicate publications, or fake engagement. Medium actively monitors for block evasion and will permanently suspend your account, voiding any paid subscriptions. Use temporary numbers only for legitimate, first-time signups.

I requested a code three times and got nothing. What is my next step?

Stop requesting codes immediately; repeated attempts can lock your IP for a few hours. Wait 30 minutes, then restart your phone and try on a different network or browser. If it still fails, use a fresh virtual number or contact Medium support with your timing details.
