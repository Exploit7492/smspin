#Why Your Vinted OTP Isn't Coming Through: Common Culprits

The main reason your code isn't arriving is usually your mobile carrier throttling or blocking Vinted's SMS gateway. This is especially common with prepaid lines, VoIP numbers like Google Voice, or a number from a different country than your Vinted region. Other common issues are a full SMS inbox, carrier-level spam filters, or just a random network delay on Vinted’s end.

  • Carrier blocks: Some budget or MVNO carriers flag verification texts as Spam and drop them.

  • Number compatibility: Vinted is picky. It often rejects disposable VoIP numbers outright. You need a real, non-VoIP local number.

  • Inbox full: Yep, your phone will silently reject new texts if the inbox is stuffed to the limit. Clear some space.

  • Regional mismatch: Using a French number for a US-based Vinted account can cause immediate delivery failures.

  • Vinted server delays: During peak sales periods or weekends, Vinted's servers get hammered, leading to 15–30-minute delays.

Carrier blocks are the #1 silent killer of Vinted OTPs. Most users never see a failure message, just an empty inbox.

#Vinted Verification Code Not Arriving? Try These 5 Fixes First

Before you spiral, run through this quick checklist. You might be surprised.

  • Wait 10 minutes. Vinted delays are annoyingly common.

  • Restart your phone. It sounds basic, but it refreshes the cellular connection.

  • Check your SMS blocked list and spam folder. Vinted's shortcode might be silently filtered.

  • Turn off Wi-Fi and use mobile data only. Some carriers route SMS differently over Wi-Fi calling.

  • Try a different number entirely. Your carrier might not support Vinted's sender ID.

  • Wait and retry. Vinted batches its SMS sends, so ten-minute delays are normal.

  • Clear your Messages app cache on Android or force-close it on iPhone.

  • If you use a dual-SIM phone, make sure the SIM you gave to Vinted is the active data line.

#Vinted OTP Not Showing Up on iPhone vs Android Device-Specific Fixes.

On iPhone, if your OTP is MIA, check if you've enabled Filter Unknown Senders in Messages settings. This feature silences texts from unknown numbers, which is where your OTP is coming from. On Android, the problem is often the Messages app not syncing with carrier SMS, or Do Not Disturb suppressing the notification.

  • Go to Settings > Messages > turn off Filter Unknown Senders and disable Silence, Unknown Callers.

  • Sign in and out of iMessage. This can force an SMS refresh.

  • Open the Messages app, tap the three dots > Settings, and verify your default SMS app is set correctly.

  • Disable Do Not Disturb and check the Spam & blocked folder in Messages by Google.

  • If all device-level tweaks fail, the issue is likely on the carrier side. Switch to a SMSPin number.

iPhone users lose OTPs to 'Filter Unknown Senders' more often than they realise that one toggle change can fix it instantly.

#Why Does Vinted SMS Verification Fail for International Users?

Vinted's SMS delivery can be notoriously spotty for international users. Its gateways are regionally optimised, so your carrier might not have a direct peering agreement with Vinted's SMS provider in your country. The message then gets bounced through multiple unreliable hops or dropped entirely.

  • Vinted mainly operates in Europe and North America. Users in Asia, Africa, or South America often face the worst delays.

  • Carriers in many countries charge high fees for receiving SMS, so Vinted may skip sending to those numbers.

  • If you're travelling, your home carrier might block international SMS from short codes as a fraud-prevention measure.

  • Using a local virtual number from your target country, e.g., a UK number for Vinted UK, eliminates routing issues.

#Troubleshooting Vinted SMS Delivery Problems: Carrier Blocks & Do Not Disturb

Carrier blocks are the #1 silent killer of Vinted OTPs. Many carriers, especially budget MVNOs, block all SMS from shortcode senders by default. Meanwhile, your phone's Do Not Disturb mode, if set to Allow only favourites, will suppress the incoming SMS notification without showing any warning.

  • Contact your carrier and ask whether they block application-to-person (A2P) SMS from short codes.

  • On Android, check the Messages app > Settings > Advanced > Auto-download files over MMS. This can interfere with SMS routing.

  • On iPhone, ensure SMS/MMS forwarding is off under Settings > Messages, as it can cause routing loops.

  • DND mode: On both platforms, open the Do Not Disturb settings and remove Messages from your exceptions during testing.

  • Still can't receive? Use SMSPin. It bypasses carrier blocks entirely because the number is hosted on their network.

#FAQ

Is using a temporary number for Vinted verification legal?

Yes, it's legal. You are simply using a genuine mobile number provided by a third-party service to receive an SMS. Vinted's terms prohibit using number-rental pools for fraud, e.g., creating multiple accounts to abuse promotions, but using a single temporary number for a single legitimate account is not a violation. Always use the number only for your own account.

Why does my Vinted OTP keep failing even with a good number?

Vinted has a built-in rate limit: you can only request 3 OTPs per phone number per hour. If you've hit that limit, you'll need to use a different number or wait 60 minutes. Also, ensure you're entering the number exactly as it appears; leading zeros can cause mismatches.

Can I use a Google Voice number for Vinted verification?

No. Google Voice numbers are VoIP-based, and Vinted blocks them at sign-up. Your number must come from a real mobile carrier MNO or an MVNO with a physical SIM. SMSPin numbers are real SIMs and work with Vinted.

What should I NOT use a temporary Vinted number for?

Do not use a temporary number to bypass Vinted's security checks on an existing account you don't control. That's account theft and is illegal. Also, don't use a disposable number for payment-recovery features; you won't get recovery codes if you lose the number.

How long does a Vinted OTP code remain valid?

The OTP code expires 3 minutes after Vinted sends it. If you don't enter it in time, you'll need to request a new one. However, don't spam the Resend button. Wait at least 60 seconds to avoid triggering Vinted's abuse detection.
