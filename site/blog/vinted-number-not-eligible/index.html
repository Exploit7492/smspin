#What Does Vinted Number Not Eligible Mean?

The "number not eligible" error on Vinted means the platform's automated system has flagged your phone number as invalid, a virtual or VoIP line, or one used too many times for account verification. This is Vinted's anti-fraud measure to prevent spam accounts and bot registrations. It's not a ban on you personally. It simply means that specific number can't be used for verification on their platform right now.

Here's what's happening behind the scenes:

  • Vinted uses third-party verification services that maintain blocklists of known virtual numbers. Many temporary or VoIP lines are automatically rejected before the OTP is ever sent.

  • The error can appear at multiple points: during registration, when you're changing your phone number, or when you're prompted to re-verify your account after suspicious activity.

  • If your number was previously used on another Vinted account, it will likely be flagged as already in use or not eligible.

  • The message is deliberately vague. Vinted doesn't tell you why the number failed because revealing that information would help fraudsters bypass the system. That's why you need to troubleshoot step by step.

#Why Vinted Rejects Your Phone Number

Vinted may reject phone numbers for several reasons, including unsupported virtual or VoIP numbers, numbers already linked to other accounts, country or region mismatches, or carrier-level filtering. Identifying the likely cause can help you choose the appropriate fix. Some users may also encounter issues with recycled numbers or numbers previously used on the platform.

The specific triggers can include:

  • Virtual number restrictions: Some virtual or VoIP numbers may not be accepted for verification. If you try free numbers, make sure the number type is supported by Vinted and permitted under its terms.

  • Country code mismatch: If your phone number's country code does not align with your account or regional settings, Vinted may not accept the number for verification.

  • Previously used numbers: Recycled numbers or numbers already associated with another Vinted account may trigger a verification error.

  • Carrier compatibility: Certain carriers or number ranges may have delivery or verification issues, so a different supported mobile number may work better.

  • Too many attempts: Repeatedly trying several numbers in a short period may trigger temporary verification restrictions. Avoid repeated attempts and wait before trying again.

#Vinted OTP Not Arriving? 5 Quick Checks Before You Panic

If your Vinted OTP (one-time password) isn't arriving, don't assume the platform is broken. Check the basics first. In most cases, the issue is a typo in your number, a slow carrier, or your phone's spam filter eating the message. Run through these five checks before you try a different number or contact support.

  • Check your number format: Ensure you're entering the full international format with the correct country code, such as +1 for the US or +44 for the UK.

  • Wait 5 to 10 minutes: SMS delivery can be delayed during peak times. Vinted's system sometimes takes a few minutes to send the code.

  • Check your spam folder: Many Android phones automatically filter SMS from unknown senders into a Spam or Blocked folder.

  • Restart your phone: A quick reboot clears temporary network glitches that can block incoming texts.

  • Try again with a fresh code: Tap resend after 60 seconds. Sometimes the carrier drops the first request.

If none of these work, the issue is likely on Vinted's side or your number is flagged. Move on to the next section.

#Vinted Phone Verification Not Working on Android? Try These Fixes

Android users face a few unique quirks when verifying Vinted, mostly related to SMS permissions and aggressive battery optimization. If the OTP won't show up on your Android device, a couple of quick settings tweaks usually solve the problem. The good news: this is rarely a Vinted issue and almost always a local phone setting.

Here's what to check on your Android device:

  • Disable battery optimization for your messaging app: Some Android phones, including Xiaomi, Samsung, and Huawei, kill background SMS processing to save battery, which can delay or block OTP delivery. Go to Settings > Battery > App Battery Optimization, find your messaging app, and set it to Unrestricted or Don't optimize.

  • Check SMS permissions: Go to Settings > Apps > Messages and make sure it has permission to receive SMS and display notifications.

  • Clear your SMS app's cache: Go to Settings > Apps > Messages > Storage > Clear Cache, then try the verification again.

  • Try the web version: If the Android app is glitchy, switch to Vinted on your mobile browser and attempt verification there.

  • Update the Vinted app: Outdated versions sometimes have bugs with SMS verification. Make sure you're on the latest build from the Google Play Store.

For official technical guidance on SMS permissions and messaging apps, refer to Android developer documentation.

Follow these best practices:

  • Use one number per account: Never reuse a number across multiple Vinted accounts. This triggers immediate flags.

  • Keep your carrier consistent: Switching between prepaid and postpaid carriers frequently can make your number look suspicious.

  • Verify your account immediately after registering: Numbers that are added days after registration are more likely to be flagged as suspicious.
