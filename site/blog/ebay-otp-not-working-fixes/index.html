#Why Your eBay OTP Isn't Arriving And What to Check First

Most of the time, eBay OTP not working comes down to three things: network delays, carrier filtering, or a phone number you typed wrong. Before you start pulling your hair out, run through these:

  • Carrier blocks: Your mobile provider might be flagging eBay's short code 7290 as spam. Yeah, that happens. Check your SMS block list and spam folder.

  • Number format: Double-check you've got the right country code +1 for US, +44 for UK and remove any leading zeros

  • Signal strength: Weak signal delays SMS. Toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds; it forces a network refresh.

  • VoIP rejection: eBay's not a fan of free VoIP numbers. If you're using a virtual number, make sure it's carrier-grade

Most eBay OTP delivery failures occur because your carrier filters messages from short codes or your number lacks the correct country prefix.

#How to Fix eBay SMS Verification Failed Errors on Desktop and Mobile.

eBay SMS verification failed usually pops up when the platform can't reach your phone or detects an incompatible number. Here's the fix:

  • Desktop: Clear browser cache and cookies. Turn off ad blockers or VPNs; they can interfere with eBay's SMS gateway. Try a different browser Chrome vs. Firefox sometimes makes a difference

  • Mobile: Make sure the eBay app has SMS permission. Force-stop the app, reopen it, and check for updates.

  • Fallback: Hit Call me during verification. eBay will ring you with the code instead. Honestly, this works more often than you'd think.

  • Session reset: Log out and wait 15 minutes before trying again. This resets rate-limiting triggers.

Still stuck? Check eBay's server status on a site like DownDetector no link here, just Google it. It could be a temporary outage on their end.

#eBay Two-Factor Authentication Not Working? Reset Your Security Settings

When eBay two-factor authentication not working appears, it's usually due to a corrupted session or outdated security settings. Annoying? Yes. Fixable? Absolutely.

  • Go to Account Settings > Security > Two-Step Verification

  • Disable two-step verification entirely

  • Re-enable it and re-register your phone number.

  • As a backup, set up an authenticator app like Google Authenticator or Authy; these generate TOTP codes offline and are never affected by carrier issues.

  • Make sure your phone's date and time are set to automatic; mismatches break TOTP generation.

If you recently switched carriers, update your number in eBay's security settings. Old carrier data can cause two-factor failures.

#Troubleshooting eBay Login Issues with OTP: Step-by-Step Guide

eBay login issues with OTP often happen after a password change or when you're logging in from a new device. Here's a step-by-step approach:

  • Wait 60 seconds before requesting a new code; rapid requests trigger eBay's rate-limiting

  • Click the Having trouble? link on the login screen to switch to email verification temporarily

  • Change networks: Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data or vice versa to bypass IP-based blocks

  • Clear eBay cookies and site data in your browser settings

  • If you're on a shared computer, make sure no other eBay session is active; log out of all devices first

If you still can't log in, hit up eBay's live chat support. They can manually trigger a code.

#eBay Verification Code Not Received After Multiple Attempts? Try This

  • Check network congestion: Try requesting at a different time of day

  • Number porting: If you recently ported your number, temporary SMS failures are common; wait 24 hours

  • For international users: Verify your carrier supports SMS from US-based short codes

#eBay SMS Confirmation Error: What It Means and How to Resolve It

An eBay SMS confirmation error means the platform couldn't deliver the code to your number due to a technical glitch or carrier incompatibility. This error often occurs with prepaid SIMs and international numbers.

Resolve it by:
