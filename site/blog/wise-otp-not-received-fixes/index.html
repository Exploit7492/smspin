TL;DR

Not receiving your Wise OTP? Learn how to troubleshoot missing verification codes, SMS delays, carrier filtering, device issues, account checks, and alternative verification options.

Getting a Wise OTP not received error is frustrating, especially when you're mid-transfer and need to get in. The good news? It's rarely a problem with your Wise account itself. More often than not, it's a device or network hiccup you can sort out in minutes.

This guide is for anyone using Wise internationally who needs a practical troubleshooting plan. We'll cover why codes go missing, how to check if your account is active, and what to do when your personal number can't receive SMS anymore.

#Quick Answer

  • Test basic SMS reception: Text a friend and have them reply.

  • Turn off Wi-Fi calling: It can silently block SMS from reaching your handset.

  • Wait before hitting Resend: Give it at least 60 seconds.

  • Expect some delay: Codes can legitimately take up to 10 minutes.

  • Consider a virtual number: If your SIM is toast, a temporary online number works reliably.

#Why Wise OTP Codes Go Missing and Why It's Usually Not Your Phone

Here's the thing most people miss: Wise OTPs often fail at the carrier level. Your mobile network might quietly drop the message. Or your phone switches to Wi-Fi calling and never picks up the SMS. The issue isn't always Wise - it's frequently your network settings doing something unexpected.

Before you blame the app, check whether your phone can receive any SMS. That simple friend-test tells you more than an hour of digging through settings.

Look for these common device-level culprits:

  • Empty carrier message center number: If your SMS center settings are incorrect, messages may fail to send or receive.

  • Wi-Fi calling enabled: Temporarily turn it off to check whether cellular SMS delivery improves.

  • Spam filters on the handset: Your phone may silently move the Wise code to a spam or blocked folder.

  • APN misconfiguration: Incorrect network settings can affect connectivity and may interfere with SMS-related services.

  • Flight mode left on: Make sure Airplane Mode is turned off so your phone can connect to the cellular network and complete SMS verification.

#Wise Account Check - How to Verify if Your Account Is Active Before Requesting a New Code

Before you pile on more fixes, confirm your account isn't locked. A quick Wise OTP account check tells you whether the problem is on your device or in the login flow itself. If your account is restricted or flagged, Wise won't even generate a code to send.

Try a basic login test:

  • Open the Wise app.

  • Attempt to log in with your email and password.

  • Watch for any error message before you reach the OTP screen.

An error about account limits or verification suggests an account-side issue. Check your email for notifications from Wise. Also, an IP mismatch - logging in from a country you don't usually use - can trigger a server-side block on sending codes. The code may be generated, but the server drops it because the session doesn't look secure.

#How to Fix Wise OTP Not Received - Quick Checks in 5 Minutes

The fix for a missing code is usually more straightforward than you'd think. These five steps resolve the Wise OTP issue for most people in under five minutes.

Work through them in order:

  • Press Resend after 60 seconds. If the button exists, the code was sent. Hitting it too fast spams the server and locks the option.

  • Disconnect from Wi-Fi. Turn off Wi-Fi and force cellular data. This creates a fresh network route for the SMS.

  • Check blocked numbers. Open your call/SMS app and see if Wise's sender ID, typically a 5-6 digit number, is on the block list.

  • Toggle airplane mode. Turn it on for 30 seconds, then off. This re-registers your phone with the mobile network and resets the SMS channel.

These steps work because they reset the SMS delivery path without requesting a new code from Wise.

#Wise OTP Contact Support - The Fastest Way to Reach a Human

If the code still isn't arriving, skip the email queue. The fastest way to reach Wise OTP support is the in-app live chat. Email takes days; live chat gets you a human in minutes.

Prepare before reaching out:

  • Your account email address.

  • Your device OS version iOS or Android.

  • Your current location, but don't volunteer it unless asked.

Open the Wise app, find Help, and select Chat with us. Phone callbacks are offered in some regions, but that's not guaranteed. If you're using a third-party app or launcher that overlays the screen, try logging in through your mobile browser instead. Can't access the app at all? Use the Wise official website support page.

#Wise Verification Code Not Arriving - Advanced Fixes

Okay, still nothing? Time for advanced fixes. A Wise verification code not arriving often comes down to how your phone formats international numbers.

Work through these in order:

  • Re-enter your phone number with + and the country code: Make sure the number uses the correct international format.

  • Force-stop the Wise app: Open your device settings, find Wise under Apps, force-stop it, clear the cache, and reopen the app.

  • Check your SIM settings: On a dual-SIM phone, make sure the SIM linked to your Wise account is active for calls and SMS.

  • Check notification settings: Make sure Wise notifications and SMS alerts are not being silenced or filtered. SMSPin may provide temporary numbers for legitimate verification where supported.

#Why Is Wise OTP Not Arriving? Error and Delay Causes

So the OTP was sent, but never arrived. What happened? It's rarely a personal block. The usual culprits are:

  • Temporary server outages: The SMS gateway is backed up and messages lag.

  • An outdated app: Your Wise app may be set up to display the OTP field, but the backend has updated, and the old version can't process the response.

  • Request frequency limits: Too many resends too fast trigger a rate limit.

  • SIM lock issues: A physically damaged or deactivated SIM can drop messages silently.

A telltale sign of a server-side issue: no error appears when you press Resend. If delays stretch past 30 minutes, check Wise's status page.

#Wise Code Delayed - Wait, Recheck, or Use Alternative Methods

Wise verification codes can be delayed by 5 to 20 minutes depending on network load. A 10-minute wait can be totally normal. But at some point, you need to stop waiting and try alternatives.

After 10 minutes, try:

  • Select the Call me option over SMS. A voice call delivers the code verbally and bypasses the SMS gateway entirely.

  • Check notifications. Sometimes a silent notification will show the code hash in the notification summary - open your message app.

  • Use a different SMS method when the app offers it. Some apps route SMS through a different gateway when you switch methods.

Also, if your carrier routes through a peer with a poor reputation, the shortcode gateway can hold messages, and they may arrive with a huge delay.

#FAQ

Can I use a temporary number for Wise?

Yes, using a temporary number to receive an OTP is legal in most places and reasonably safe - but check Wise's Terms of Service first. Don't use it for fraud or to bypass security features. Respect local laws and the platform's rules.

Why does my Wise code never arrive?

Your phone's blocked messages list, carrier-level filtering, or internet calling issues can cause it. Wait for 3 minutes, try again, then double-check your device's SMS capabilities. Sometimes the network blocks messages.

Can I use a one-time number for a Wise account, or do I need to rent one?

For a one-time link, use the pay-per-use method. If your account is real and you expect codes regularly, consider renting. But don't use temporary numbers beyond your need - you'll lose access within the rental period.

What should I NOT use temporary numbers for?

Don't use them for recovery of active authentication steps, fraud, or creating multiple abusive accounts. And never store cards or make transactions tied to a virtual number - once it expires, you can lose access to those.

I think Wise sent the code, but it didn't arrive. What next?

First, check the block list. Switch to mobile data instead of Wi-Fi. If nothing arrives in 10 minutes, try a voice callback or resend the code. If it still doesn't work, use live chat support to verify your account details are current.
