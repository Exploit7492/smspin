#Why Is My Flipkart Verification Code Not Coming?

An OTP not arriving is usually caused by network congestion, carrier filtering, or app-side delays. Flipkart depends on operator gateways that may slow down during busy periods. Check your SMS inbox, turn off spam filters, and clear blocked messages before requesting a new SMS verification code. 

  • Network outages or poor signal strength can prevent the SMS from hitting your phone.

  • Some telecom operators flag Flipkart's messages as spam or block them entirely.

  • Your phone's built-in SMS blocking apps or third-party number filters might catch the OTP.

  • Flipkart's servers can experience short delays that make the code appear late.

If your Flipkart verification code isn't coming through, the first thing to do is rule out a phone-level filter. OTPs are sent via standard SMS gateways, but delays of 2–5 minutes are common during peak hours.

#How to Solve the Flipkart OTP Not Received Issue

Here's the fastest checklist to get your code back on track. Restart your phone, toggle airplane mode, and check your SMS filter settings. If that doesn't work, request a new OTP after 5 minutes or try using a different SIM card if you have one available.

Immediate steps to solve the Flipkart OTP not received issue:

  • Power cycle your device to refresh the mobile network connection to the tower.

  • Temporarily turn off SMS blockers or spam protection to let the message through.

  • Ensure your SIM card is active and has a strong signal; try it in another phone to confirm.

  • Wait at least 2 minutes before re-requesting the OTP to avoid rate-limiting by Flipkart.

  • Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data; some VoIP connections interfere with SMS delivery.

If these steps don't resolve it, the problem is deeper: your carrier is likely blocking the short-code. That's when a virtual number becomes the best way to solve the Flipkart OTP not received issue.

#Flipkart Mobile Number Verification Failed? Don't Panic – Try These Steps

A  verification failed error typically means the OTP expired or the number you entered has a formatting error. Double-check your country code and remove any leading zeroes from your mobile number. If the error persists, it's often a carrier-side blocking issue rather than a Flipkart problem.

  • Remove any dashes, spaces, or plus signs in the phone number field plain digits only.

  • Verify that your SIM is in a phone with SMS capability, not just data-only.

  • Try the verification on a different device or browser to rule out cookie corruption.

  • Consider using a different mobile number (temporary or virtual) if the failure continues.

#What to Do When Flipkart SMS Is Not Delivered to Your Number

When the SMS just never shows up, the problem is almost always on the delivery path, not your phone. Check if you're in a do-not-disturb (DND) list that blocks promotional or transactional SMS. You can also ask your carrier to unblock short-code numbers used by ecommerce platforms.

  • Call your mobile operator to confirm they're not blocking short-code SMS from Flipkart.

  • Check if you recently ported your number; ported numbers sometimes miss OTPs for a few hours.

  • Ensure your phone's messaging app hasn't changed the default SMS app after an update.

  • If using a dual-SIM phone, confirm the OTP is being sent to the correct SIM slot.

Experiencing Flipkart SMS not delivered after following all checks? The carrier fix may take hours. A faster workaround is to switch to a virtual number that guarantees delivery.
