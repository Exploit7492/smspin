Hitting that "LINE verification temporarily blocked" message right when you need to get into your account? Yeah, it's annoying. Whether you're setting up a fresh profile or trying to recover an old one, having your phone number suddenly rejected feels like hitting a brick wall. This guide walks through why LINE blocks numbers, what actually works to fix it, and how to avoid getting stuck again.

  • "Temporarily Blocked" means LINE's system flagged your number for something suspicious—such as rapid re-registrations or the use of shared virtual numbers.

  • Wait it out: Blocks typically last 24–72 hours, but that won't help if the number is blocked.

#Why LINE Blocks Your Phone Number During Verification (And What "Temporarily Blocked" Really Means)

"SMSPin is not affiliated with any app or website. Please follow each app's terms and local regulations."

LINE isn't just being difficult—it's built to stop spam, fraud, and abuse. It uses smart algorithms to spot and flag suspicious numbers. When you see "LINE verification temporarily blocked," it means the system has placed your number on a temporary hold. Most of the time, this isn't a permanent ban—it's an automated reaction to perceived risk.

LINE analyzes carrier data, your IP address, and behavioural patterns to determine whether a number is trustworthy. Temporary blocks usually last 24 to 72 hours, but they can recur if the root cause isn't addressed. Numbers tied to VoIP services, "free SMS" sites, or those with high re-registration rates are more likely to get flagged early. It's similar to how "SMS verification for WhatsApp" might treat certain numbers.

#The Most Common Reasons Behind "LINE Verification Denied" or "Invalid Number" Errors

When LINE shows "Verification Denied" or "Invalid Number," it indicates specific issues with the phone number itself. These errors usually boil down to three main categories: previous usage, carrier flags, or system misidentification.

Quite often, the number was already used for a LINE account and is still tied to it. Or maybe it belongs to a carrier region that LINE has restricted due to past abuse. LINE might also mistakenly identify a number as a landline instead of a mobile line. A less common but real reason is that the number sits on a telecom blocklist for SMS abuse. Numbers from "burner app" services and "free number" aggregators frequently end up on LINE's deny list.

#How to Fix "LINE Verification Code Not Received" After Your Number Gets Blocked

If your number is on a temporary hold, LINE won't send the verification code. The answer isn't to keep smashing the resend button—that can actually make things worse. Instead, you need to either wait out the block or, more practically, switch to a clean number from a different pool or country that LINE hasn't flagged yet.

Sometimes SMS delivery failures happen at the carrier level. But a "code not received" message paired with a "LINE verification temporarily blocked" alert is usually number-specific. Trying a different device or SIM rarely helps when the number itself is flagged. Using a fresh, real-time rented number from a verified pool can often bypass this lock entirely.

#Recovering a LINE Account Blocked by Verification Issues

If your LINE account is already blocked due to verification issues, recovery requires a specific approach. You'll generally need to verify using the same device and phone number you originally used to register.

Recovery Steps:

  • Wait and Retry: If the block is "temporary," give it 24-72 hours before trying again. This can resolve a soft block.

  • Original Number Access: Make sure you still have access to the original phone number linked to the account. If that number is gone, recovery gets much harder.

  • Contact LINE Support: If the original number isn't available or repeated attempts fail, your only option is to contact LINE support directly. Provide proof of registration—like a linked email, purchase history, or connected Facebook account.

  • Proof of Ownership: Be ready to share whatever information LINE requests to prove you own the account.

One thing to note: if you used a temporary number that's since expired, recovering the account is extremely tough. Prevention—using a reliable long-term number—is always better than trying to recover.

#When LINE Verification is "Unavailable" – Is It the App or Your Number?

The "LINE verification unavailable" message can be confusing. It usually means the chosen verification method (SMS or call) is temporarily disabled for your region or mobile provider, or that LINE's SMS gateway is experiencing high traffic. This doesn't necessarily mean your number is bad—it's more of a bottleneck in the delivery system.

This error occurs more often during peak hours, especially in high-demand regions such as Asia and the Middle East. Trying a number from a different, supported country—like receiving SMS in "India"—can often restore the verification option. Renting a number for 24 hours can also boost your success rate compared to using one-off numbers, since it suggests stability to LINE's system.

#How to Properly Switch Phone Numbers Without Triggering a LINE Block

LINE strongly links your phone number to your account identity for security. Trying to switch numbers without following the official in-app procedure can easily lock or flag your account.

Recommendations for Switching Numbers:

  • Use In-App Feature: Always go to LINE settings and use the "Change Phone Number" option. Verify the new number within the app before removing the old one.

  • Link Backups First: Before any number change, link your LINE account to your email address and/or Facebook profile. This gives you alternative recovery paths if things go wrong.

  • Avoid Immediate New Account Use: If you're trying to register a new LINE account with a number that was previously tied to another profile, expect an immediate block. LINE catches this quickly.

  • Patience for Temporary Lines: If you're testing with temporary lines, wait at least 48 hours between switching numbers to avoid triggering automated blocking algorithms.

#Quick Fixes vs. Long-Term Solutions: Stopping the "Temporary Verification Block" Cycle

When facing a LINE temporary verification block, it helps to know the difference between quick fixes and real long-term solutions. Quick fixes like clearing your app cache, switching Wi-Fi networks, or using a VPN rarely fix a number-specific block—because the problem is the number, not your device or network.

Long-term solutions require changing your verification strategy:

  • Fresh, Clean Number: Always pick a new number that hasn't been heavily used for LINE verifications before.

  • Avoid Reuse: Don't reuse the same number across multiple LINE accounts, or repeatedly if it's already been flagged. Retrying the same number actually reinforces the flag.

  • Dedicated Rental: For accounts you plan to keep and log into regularly, consider a long-term rental number. This gives you a stable, dedicated LINE.

  • API Integration: For advanced users or testers, using a developer API to check OTP status can reduce frustration. You can see if LINE sent the code even if your app isn't showing it, giving you clearer insight into whether the number is blocked or just having delivery issues.

#What To Do If You're Still Getting Blocked on LINE Verification (Final Checklist)

If you've tried everything above and still keep hitting "LINE verification temporarily blocked" or "LINE verification denied phone number," it's time for a final, systematic approach.

Final Checklist for Persistent Blocks:

  • Physically Different Device: Try a different smartphone or tablet for verification. Sometimes device fingerprints or IP addresses tied to past suspicious activity can trigger blocks. Make sure you're not using a compromised or flagged device.

  • Number from a Different Country/Provider: Get a number from an entirely different country or a known reliable mobile provider. LINE's blocking algorithms often target specific regions or virtual number providers.

  • Brand New Number Guarantee: Confirm the number you're using has genuinely never been linked to a LINE account before. Even paid temporary numbers sometimes get recycled.

  • IP Address Match: Check if your IP address broadly matches the geographical region of your number. LINE sometimes cross-references this for fraud detection.

  • Pace Your Attempts: Don't try more than 2-3 verifications per hour from the same device. Rapid-fire retries are a major red flag for LINE's anti-spam systems.

  • Provider's Number Pool: If you keep getting blocks with different numbers from a paid SMS service, the provider itself might need to refresh its number pool. Request a refund for failed attempts and consider switching providers.

#Key Takeaways:

  • "LINE verification temporarily blocked" is a system flag, usually for 24-72 hours, triggered by suspicious activity like number reuse or VoIP detection.

  • Common errors like "Verification Denied" or "Invalid Number" indicate that LINE likely blocks your number due to past use, your carrier type, or its listing in spam databases.

  • To fix "Code Not Received" after a block, waiting or (preferably) switching to a fresh, unflagged number is key; retrying the same number backfires.

  • For account recovery, use your original device and number, or contact LINE support with proof of ownership. Recovery is tough if the original temporary number is lost.

  • "Verification Unavailable" usually points to carrier/regional congestion, not a bad number; trying a number from a different country can resolve it.

#FAQ

Is it legal to use a temporary phone number for LINE verification? 

Yes, it's legal as long as you're not using it to break LINE's terms of service (like creating fake accounts, spamming, or evading bans). Use temporary numbers for privacy or legitimate testing only.

Why does LINE say "this number is invalid" when it works for other apps? 

LINE keeps its own blocklist of numbers, which often includes lines from known virtual number providers, prepaid SIMs, or numbers that have been used too many times. A number might work perfectly on WhatsApp or Telegram but get blocked on LINE.

Should I use a one-time SMS code or a rental number for LINE? 

For a single verification with no plans to keep the account, a one-time code works fine. If you intend to log in again or maintain the account, renting a number for 3–30 days is much safer and less likely to trigger a future block.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for on LINE? 

Don't use a temporary number for LINE Pay, identity verification, backup email setup, or any financial feature. Temporary numbers should be used only for initial registration or for privacy-protected messaging.

Will waiting 24 hours fix my "LINE verification temporarily blocked" error? 

Sometimes, yes—LINE applies a 24–72-hour cooldown to flagged numbers. But if the underlying number is blocked, waiting won't help. Your best bet is to try a different, clean number.

Why didn't I receive the LINE verification code even though SMS works on my phone? 

LINE's SMS gateway might be rate-limiting your region, or your carrier could be blocking SMS from LINE's short code. This is a delivery issue, not a number issue—try a different carrier number or request a voice call instead.

Can I recover my LINE account if I deleted the app and forgot my password? Yes, if you still have access to the original phone number. If you used a temporary number that's expired, recovery becomes very difficult. Always link your LINE account to an email or Facebook account to create a backup recovery path.
