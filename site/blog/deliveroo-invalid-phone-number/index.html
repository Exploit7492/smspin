Deliveroo Invalid Phone Number Error: How to Fix

TL;DR

Getting the Deliveroo invalid phone number error? Learn how to fix formatting, country code mismatches, unsupported number types, account associations, SMS verification issues, app problems, and regional restrictions.

Let's cut straight to it: Deliveroo's invalid phone number error is almost never a genuine typo on your end but it's not random either. It usually comes down to a country-code problem, a line-type issue, or something about your account role. Each of those has a specific fix.

Who this is for: restaurant owners onboarding with Deliveroo, couriers solo or fleet-managed, and business users running a Deliveroo partner account. If you're a regular customer hitting this on sign-up, the quick fix and checklist below will sort you out too.

#Quick Answer:

  • Formatting: You're missing the +[country code] prefix, or you included spaces and dashes Deliveroo rejects that outright.

  • Region mismatch: Your account's region doesn't line up with your number's country code e.g., a UK number on a Singapore account.

  • Line type: You're using a VoIP, landline, or virtual number that Deliveroo's backend flags as invalid for SMS delivery.

  • Duplicate association: The number is already tied to another Deliveroo account the system just shows invalid instead of already in use.

#Why Does Deliveroo Say Invalid Phone Number?

Deliveroo validates phone numbers against strict formatting rules and a database of known line types. The error usually fires because the number is missing a country code, has spaces or dashes that Deliveroo's parser rejects, or sits on a VoIP/online number list that Deliveroo's security layer flags. Figuring out which reason triggered the error tells you which fix actually works.

  • Formatting rejects: Deliveroo requires full international format a + followed by the country code and the number without any leading zeros e.g., +44 7911 123456 for a UK mobile. Local formats like 07911 123456 just won't fly.

  • Line type flags: Deliveroo may cross-check numbers against known VoIP or burner ranges. If your number is flagged, the platform may block it even when the number is technically valid. SMSPin may provide temporary numbers for legitimate verification where supported.

  • Account role matters: The same number might pass for a customer account but fail on restaurant or courier onboarding. Commercial accounts get stricter checks because OTPs protect payout data. It's all part of the platform's broader phone verification process.

  • Region locking: Entering a number from a country Deliveroo doesn't support in your current region triggers a format-stage rejection.

#The 30-Second Formatting Check That Solves Most Errors

Before you go deeper, try this: type your number as +[country code][full number without leading zero], no spaces, no dashes, no parentheses. That single change clears up most Deliveroo invalid number errors because the platform's validator only accepts international format.

  • Drop the local prefix: If your number is 020 7946 0958 London, enter +442079460958 not 02079460958, and definitely not +44 020 7946 0958.

  • No spaces, ever: Some forms especially the partner portal strip spaces automatically; others don't. Enter the number as one continuous string.

  • Check the country code dropdown: Many Deliveroo forms auto-select a country code from your IP or browser locale. If the dropdown says +1 US but you're entering a UK number, you'll get the error.

  • Test with your own SIM first: If possible, restart the flow with your personal mobile number to confirm the form itself works that isolates whether the issue is your number or the platform.

#Deliveroo Restaurant Account Phone Error: What's Different for Business Accounts

Restaurant and business accounts face stricter phone validation than customer accounts because the number connects to payout details, order notifications, and multi-user access. The most common error on restaurant accounts comes from entering a landline format that Deliveroo rejects, or from reusing a number already registered to another Deliveroo business entity.

  • Landline vs. mobile: Deliveroo restaurant onboarding accepts landlines for the primary contact in some regions, but the verification OTP still requires a mobile-capable number. If your restaurant only has a landline, the verification step fails.

  • Duplicate number lockout: Each phone number can typically only be linked to one active Deliveroo restaurant account at a time. If the owner or manager already used that number on a previous account, it shows invalid rather than already in use.

  • Manager vs. owner permissions: Restaurant accounts may have role-based access, and the manager onboarding screen may reject numbers that the owner’s screen accepts. Try using the account owner’s credentials and retry SMS verification.

  • Payout region mismatch: If your restaurant's billing address is in one country but the phone number is from another, the platform flags the inconsistency.

  • Try the partner portal first: Deliveroo's restaurant onboarding is web-based. Use a desktop browser, not the mobile app, for initial validation the app's keyboard and autofill often introduce formatting errors.

#Deliveroo Courier Invalid Number: Fleet and Driver Account Fixes

Courier accounts handle phone verification differently than restaurant accounts the number is your primary identity factor for the Deliveroo Rider app, and it's checked against a separate validation system at onboarding. When couriers hit the invalid number error, it's often because they're entering a prepaid SIM number that Deliveroo's fleet system rejects, or because they're using a number already linked to a previous courier profile.

  • Prepaid SIM issues: Deliveroo's courier verification sometimes rejects numbers from prepaid SIM networks if the carrier's number block is flagged as high-risk. This is region-dependent.

  • Fleet-managed accounts: If a fleet manager adds you, they may enter your number in their portal with a different format than the Rider app expects. Ask the fleet admin to delete and re-add you with the +[country code] format.

  • Coverage check: Couriers in regions where Deliveroo operates but your mobile carrier has poor SMS delivery can hit invalid number because the platform fails a delivery test check.

  • Rider app vs. onboarding web form: The Rider app sometimes errors on numbers that pass the web onboarding form. If the app says invalid, try the Deliveroo courier web portal instead.

  • Previous account data: Returning riders often have stale numbers in the system. Contact Deliveroo support's courier team to purge the old record before re-verifying.

#Deliveroo Partner App Invalid Number: Where the Error Happens Matters

The Deliveroo Partner app may handle phone number validation differently from the customer app. If the Partner app rejects your number while the web portal accepts it, try entering the number manually instead of using autofill or keyboard suggestions. A free number may also be an option if Deliveroo supports that number type.

  • Autofill corruption: iOS and Android autofill often prepend or strip the country code. Clear the field, type slowly, and bypass autofill entirely.

  • Partner app cache issues: If you recently updated the app and suddenly get invalid number errors on numbers that worked before, clear the app cache stale validation rules linger.

  • Web portal as the reference: Deliveroo's partner support team treats the web portal as the source of truth. If the portal accepts your number, log out of the app, re-log in, and re-verify. When in doubt, consult Deliveroo's official help center for platform-specific guidance.

  • App version quirks: Older Partner app versions pre-2023 had known bugs with + prefix handling. Update the app before troubleshooting the number.

  • Session expiry masking: Sometimes the invalid number error in the Partner app is actually a session timeout display bug. Force-closing and reopening the app fixes it.

#Country Code and Regional Number Issues

Deliveroo operates across dozens of countries, but its phone validator runs from a single backend which means a number that's valid in your home country may be rejected if your account region points elsewhere. The fix isn't to change your number; it's to make sure the region on your account matches the country code of the number you're entering.

  • Account region vs. number country: If your Deliveroo account is set to Singapore but you're entering a UK number, the system will flag it. Change the account region first, or use a local number.

  • Country code prefix variations: Some countries have multiple valid prefixes e.g., +44 and +440 are not interchangeable. Deliveroo accepts only the +44 format without the internal zero. This follows the ITU International Telecommunication Union E.164 number format standard.

  • Non-geographic numbers: UK 03xx numbers, US 800 numbers, or any non-geographic service number get rejected because SMS delivery to them is unreliable.

  • Roaming numbers: If you're a courier or restaurant owner abroad using your home SIM on roaming, SMS delivery timeouts due to roaming delays can trigger the invalid message even though the format is correct.

  • Dual-SIM confusion: Phones with dual SIMs sometimes send the OTP via the wrong SIM, which makes the platform think the number is unreachable and that surfaces as invalid number rather than delivery failed.

#Deliveroo Phone Verification Failed: Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

When Deliveroo says phone verification failed, it means the platform accepted your format but the verification step itself didn't complete either the OTP never arrived, arrived too late, or the code was entered incorrectly. That's a distinct error from the invalid number message.

  • Check spam and SMS folders: Some Android phones file OTP messages under Transactions or separate tabs. The code may have arrived you just missed it.

  • Request a resend, don't retype: After one failed attempt, the code expires. Request a fresh OTP rather than trying the old one.

  • Voice call fallback: Some Deliveroo flows may offer a voice call if SMS doesn’t arrive. If your number supports calls but can’t receive SMS, try the voice option instead.

  • Wait 10 minutes between attempts: Deliveroo rate-limits verification attempts. Too many tries within a short window will fail even with a valid number.

  • Test SMS delivery independently: Send yourself a text from another phone. If it doesn't arrive, your carrier or the number's line type is the bottleneck not Deliveroo.

#Deliveroo App vs. Site Phone Problem: Which Platform Should You Use?

Deliveroo's mobile app and website use separate validation flows, and they don't always behave identically. In practice, the web version accessed via desktop browser is more permissive and more reliable for phone number entry it lacks the autofill, keyboard, and SMS interception quirks that plague mobile forms.

  • App-side autofill garbage: Mobile autofill frequently inserts numbers with dashes or spaces, which the app then fails to parse the website forces a single continuous field, avoiding this.

  • SMS interception differences: Android's SMS Retriever API used by the Deliveroo app sometimes fails silently while the web version's manual entry works. The code arrives; the app just doesn't pick it up.

  • Browser locale overrides: On the web, your browser's language and locale can incorrectly set the country prefix. Clear cookies or use incognito mode if you see a pre-filled country code you didn't select.

  • Desktop is the debugging tool: If a number fails everywhere, the desktop browser's console Network tab shows you the exact error response which tells you whether it's a format reject or a delivery check failure.

  • When to use the app: After the number is verified and active, the app is fine for ongoing use. The verification step specifically is where the website wins.

#SMS Verification Issues: Why the Code Never Arrives And What to Do

Even when Deliveroo accepts your number format, you can still hit the invalid number or verification failed message if the OTP never actually lands in your SMS inbox. This is especially common with virtual numbers, VoIP lines, and numbers from carriers with poor international SMS routing. If your number passes the format check but the code never arrives, the problem is the number's actual SMS capability, not Deliveroo's validation logic.

  • VoIP and VoIP-adjacent numbers fail: Deliveroo's backend actively flags numbers that lack traditional SMS support e.g., Skype numbers, some Google Voice variants, certain VoIP providers. These won't work.

  • Carrier SMS routing delays: Some international carriers have 5–15 minute SMS delays. Deliveroo's OTP expires faster than that, so the code arrives after it's already dead which surfaces as a verification failure. This aligns with carrier SMS delivery best practices docs on routing reliability.

  • Number recycling risks: If your number was previously used for a Deliveroo account or other spam-flagged services, the platform may silently block OTP delivery even though the format checks out.

  • Temporary numbers that pass but don't deliver: Some virtual number providers claim SMS reception but route through email-to-SMS gateways that Deliveroo rejects. Test with a service that guarantees real SMS reception before blaming Deliveroo.

  • The fix path: Use a number from a major carrier Vodafone, EE, O2, T-Mobile, Verizon, etc. that you can physically receive SMS on in the same region as your account. If your code never arrives, use a different number for verification that's built for reliable SMS delivery.

#Using a Different Number: When and How to Switch Your Deliveroo Contact Number

Sometimes the fastest fix for a persistent invalid number error is to use a different number entirely whether that's a second personal SIM, a family member's number, or a temporary virtual number for testing. The key rule: the replacement number must be SMS-capable, in the same country as your account region, and not already linked to another Deliveroo account.

  • When to switch: If you've tried formatting fixes, country code changes, and browser troubleshooting with zero progress, the number itself is the problem.

  • Temporary vs. permanent: Use a temporary number to complete verification, then update to your permanent business number after the account is active. Deliveroo allows contact number changes post-verification.

  • Second SIM strategy: If you need a number that receives SMS reliably but you don't want to expose your personal number, a cheap prepaid SIM used only for OTPs works just ensure it's from a recognized carrier, not a VoIP provider.

  • The one-number-per-account rule: Don't reuse a number from a previous Deliveroo account. That association is the cause of the invalid flag in the first place.

  • Post-verification update path: After your account is live, update the phone number in the account settings to your real business or personal number. If you're exploring cost-effective options, check our live pricing per country to keep testing affordable.

#Temporary Numbers for Deliveroo Testing: What Works and What Doesn't

Using a temporary virtual number for Deliveroo testing is possible, but you need to be careful: Deliveroo's backend flags many VoIP numbers as invalid, and a number that doesn't actually receive SMS won't help you verify anything. For testing purposes e.g., testing your own restaurant's order flow, or testing the Deliveroo app without exposing your SIM, a temporary number that supports real SMS reception works for verification but you should not rely on it for ongoing account use. Temporary numbers are a testing tool, not an account solution.

  • What works: Temporary numbers from services that use real SIM hardware or carrier-grade SMS routing not email-to-SMS can pass Deliveroo's format check and receive OTPs. Check out per-use temporary numbers that prioritize carrier reliability.

  • What fails: VoIP lines, most free SMS-receiving websites, and numbers from providers with poor international SMS routing will hit the invalid number error or fail at the OTP delivery step.

  • Region matching: The temporary number's country code must match your account region. A UK temporary number won't verify a US Deliveroo account.

  • Use case clarity: For a one-time test, a temporary number is fine. For restaurant or courier business accounts, use a permanent number Deliveroo runs ongoing verification checks that temporary numbers can fail.

  • Cost reality: Free SMS-receiving sites almost always fail Deliveroo verification. Paid, per-use temporary numbers have a higher chance of success because they use real carrier infrastructure.

#Preventing Future Invalid Number Errors on Your Deliveroo Account

Once you've fixed your current invalid number error, the next step is keeping it from recurring. The golden rules: keep your account's region in sync with your phone number's country, never reuse a number that's been linked to another Deliveroo account, and avoid number changes unless absolutely necessary each change triggers a fresh validation cycle that can re-introduce the error if you're not careful.

  • The one-number rule: For restaurants and couriers, designate a single person the account owner whose number is the primary contact. Don't rotate numbers between team members.

  • Change management: When you must change a number, do it once, verify immediately, and confirm the new number receives SMS before updating the account. Don't leave the account in a half-updated state.

  • SIM and carrier stability: Don't port numbers or switch carriers close to a Deliveroo verification event. Porting windows can make your number unreachable for voice and SMS.

  • Monitor for silent failures: If you stop receiving Deliveroo notifications but don't see an error, the number may have been flagged. Schedule a bi-monthly test: request a password reset to confirm SMS delivery still works.

#Key Takeaways

  • Account type matters: Restaurant, courier, and customer accounts validate numbers differently. Business accounts face stricter checks for duplicate association, line type, and payout region consistency.

  • The Deliveroo website accepts numbers the app may reject due to autofill corruption, SMS interceptor failures, or app cache issues always try the web portal first for verification.

#FAQ

Is it legal to use a temporary number for Deliveroo verification?

Using a temporary number for legitimate verification can be legal, but you should follow Deliveroo's current terms and applicable local regulations. Temporary numbers shouldn't be used to create abusive accounts, bypass restrictions, or engage in fraud. For business or courier accounts that require reliable long-term contact, a permanent number is generally more appropriate.

Why does my valid phone number show as invalid on Deliveroo?

A valid number can still be rejected because of an incorrect country code, country or region mismatch, unsupported number type, previous account association, or other verification checks. Enter the number in the required international format and make sure the selected country matches the number.

What's the difference between a one-time temporary number and a rental number for Deliveroo?

A one-time number is intended for a single verification and may become unavailable afterward. A rental number stays active for a longer period, which can be useful when you legitimately need continued access to SMS messages during a testing period. Choose the option based on how long you need the number.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for on Deliveroo?

Don't use a temporary number for business or courier accounts that require reliable long-term contact if you may lose access to the number. You should also never use temporary numbers to create abusive accounts, bypass restrictions, evade bans, or engage in fraud or other activity that violates Deliveroo's terms.

The code never arrives on my temporary number. What am I doing wrong?

Check that the number is active, can receive SMS, and is supported by Deliveroo. SMS delivery can be affected by carrier filtering, routing problems, unsupported number types, or temporary platform restrictions. Make sure the number's country matches the country selected on Deliveroo, and avoid repeatedly requesting new codes if delivery is delayed.

Why does the same number work on the Deliveroo website but fail in the app?

The difference can be caused by app-specific issues such as cached data, outdated software, or problems with the way the mobile app processes the number field. Update the app, clear its cache where applicable, restart it, and try entering the number manually. If the issue continues, try the web version or contact Deliveroo Support.

Can I change my Deliveroo number after verification without re-triggering the invalid number check?

Changing your phone number may require the new number to go through Deliveroo's verification process again. Use the correct country and international format, and make sure the new number can receive SMS. If the number is already associated with another account or isn't supported, the update may be rejected.
