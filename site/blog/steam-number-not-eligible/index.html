#Why Is My Phone Number Not Eligible for Steam? (Common Causes)

Steam's number not eligible error usually means your phone number is already linked to another Steam account, belongs to a VoIP/Virtual carrier that Steam has blocked, or uses a country code that Steam doesn't fully support. The system also flags numbers that are used for too many verification attempts within a short window. Understanding which block you're hitting is the first step to fixing it.

Here's what typically triggers it:

  • Already-linked numbers: Steam ties one number to one account. If you or someone else used it before, it's locked.

  • Carrier block: Numbers from Google Voice, TextNow, or other free VoIP providers are often automatically rejected.

  • Rate limiting: Entering the wrong code or requesting too many SMS in 24 hours triggers a temporary ban on that number.

  • Country restrictions: Some regions have limited Steam SMS support, especially in parts of Asia and Africa.

For more detailed information, refer to the Steam Support – Account Phone Number FAQ.

#Steam Invalid Phone Number – What It Means and How to Fix It

The invalid phone number prompt appears when Steam can't format or route your number to its SMS gateway. This often comes down to omitting the country code, entering a number with dashes/spaces, or using a number type Steam doesn't recognize (such as a premium-rate line). The fix is simple: enter your full number in international format starting with + and the country code.

  • Line type matters: Landlines and premium numbers fail immediately. Stick to standard mobile numbers.

  • Trial and error: Try removing leading zeros from the area code. Some systems trip on redundant zeros.

For international number format guidance, check the ICANN – International Number Format Guide.

#How to Fix Steam Phone Number Verification Failed

When Steam says verification failed mid-process, it usually means the code expired (OTPs are valid for only a few minutes) or the number was flagged during the check. Start by requesting a new code after 60 seconds. If it fails again, switch to a different number entirely   one that hasn't been used for Steam verification recently.

Follow these steps in order:

  • Step 1: Wait 60–90 seconds and resend the code before touching any settings.

  • Step 2: Clear your browser cache or try a different browser/Steam client session.

  • Step 3: Use a fresh number from a pool that isn't burned out by prior Steam attempts.

  • Step 4: Check whether your country supports Steam SMS (the Steam Support site lists supported regions).

  • Step 5: If using a shared or rental number, ensure you have exclusive access during the verification window.

#What to Do When Your Steam Verification Code Is Not Received

If Steam says it sent the code but nothing arrives, first check your SMS inbox (including spam/junk folders). Then confirm your phone has a signal and hasn't blocked unknown senders. If the code still doesn't arrive within 3 minutes, the number itself may be blocked, or the SMS gateway may be overloaded. Request a new code   sometimes the second attempt routes through a different carrier.

  • Carrier filtering: Some mobile carriers flag Steam's SMS shortcodes as spam. Call your carrier to allow them if it's a recurring issue.

  • Number blocklist: If you're using a recycled number, Steam may have blocked it from receiving future codes.

  • Timeout: OTP windows are tight (2–5 minutes). Request codes only when you're ready to enter them.

For more on SMS shortcodes, refer to the FCC's Understanding SMS Short Codes.

#The Real Reason Steam Rejects Temporary or Virtual Phone Numbers

Steam actively blocks most virtual and temporary phone numbers because its system flags numbers from known VoIP blocks or pools used for mass account creation. Their fraud detection looks at the number's carrier type, historical usage, and the number of Steam accounts it's touched. That's why free virtual numbers from most apps fail   but a dedicated temporary number from a verification service can sometimes work if the number hasn't been burned yet.

  • Carrier detection: Steam checks the number's MNC/MCC against a database of VoIP and virtual carriers.

  • Burn factor: Once a number is used for Steam verification, it's often marked and may not work again.

  • Not all temp numbers are equal: Numbers from verified carriers (real mobile towers) pass more checks than free app numbers.

  • Workaround: Rent a number for a day or month to reduce the chance of it being flagged as a one-shot burner.

#Troubleshooting Steam Account Verification Trouble (Quick Fixes)

Before diving into new numbers, run through these five quick fixes: clear your Steam browser cache, try a different device (phone vs. desktop), ensure your system clock is correct (OTP codes are time-sensitive), disable any VPN that might change your IP region, and wait 30 minutes if you've had multiple failed attempts. Many verification trouble issues are actually account locks from too many rapid attempts.

  • Clock sync: An incorrect device time can invalidate time-based OTPs.

  • VPN interference: Steam's geo-detection may block verification if your IP doesn't match your number's country.

  • Cooldown timer: After 3 failed attempts, Steam imposes a 15–30 minute block. Wait it out.

  • Account age: New accounts often face stricter verification checks than established ones.

For security best practices, refer to NIST's OTP Security Best Practices.

Steam rejects numbers that are already linked to another account, come from VoIP carriers, or have incorrect formatting. Make sure you include the country code with a + prefix and no spaces.
