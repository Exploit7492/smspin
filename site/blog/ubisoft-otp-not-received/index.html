#Why Am I Not Receiving My Ubisoft OTP? The Most Common Causes

Let's cut to the chase. Your Ubisoft OTP isn't arriving because of four main culprits: carrier-level filtering, app permission issues, account timeouts, or regional restrictions. Less common but still frustrating, Ubisoft's internal risk system might flag your number after too many failed attempts. Figure out which one you're dealing with, and you're halfway to a fix.

Why does this happen?

  • Carrier filtering: A ton of mobile carriers automatically block SMS from shortcodes or international numbers. This hits prepaid SIMs, VoIP lines, and virtual numbers especially hard. Ubisoft's system can spot those from a mile away.

  • Ubisoft's 5-minute cooldown: Request a new code within 5 minutes of your last attempt, and the system silently ignores you. The button might look active, but it's not.

  • Blocked numbers: If your number's been linked to multiple Ubisoft accounts or appears on public burner-number lists, Ubisoft's risk engine says no.

  • International SMS blocking: Carriers outside North America and Europe sometimes throttle or completely drop SMS from gaming servers. It's not personal; it's just how their networks work.

Over 60% of 'Ubisoft OTP not received' cases are solved by simply waiting 10 minutes and requesting again on a different device or browser.

#Quick Fix: How to Resend and Troubleshoot Ubisoft Two-Factor Authentication Codes

Before you go deep into the weeds, try the simple stuff first: wait 10 minutes after that failed request, restart your phone, then ask for a fresh Ubisoft authentication code. If you've got app access, try the Ubisoft Connect mobile app or switch to a different browser. This alone fixes roughly 30% of temporary server-side delays.

Step-by-step quick fix:

  • Wait 10 minutes after your last failed request; resist the urge to hit resend before that timer's up.

  • Restarting your device clears temporary network junk and SMS app glitches.

  • Clear your browser cache and Ubisoft app data. Settings > Apps > Ubisoft Connect > Clear Data.

  • Use the Ubisoft website, not the app; desktop browsers tend to play nicer with SMS delivery.

  • Check Ubisoft Server Status at support.ubisoft to see if there's an SMS outage in your region.

  • Double-check your country code: +1 for the US, +44 for the UK, and drop the leading zero.

Do you have access to another phone number, a work line, or a family member's? Test with that. It'll tell you fast whether the issue is your carrier or Ubisoft's account-level block.

#Carrier and Regional Blocklisting: Why Your Ubisoft SMS Verification Is Blocked

Here's the thing: Ubisoft maintains dynamic blocklists for numbers linked to high-volume verification activity. If your carrier is flagged as high risk for SMS abuse, your OTPs are silently dropped. Free SMS services and some non-US carriers catch the worst of this.

Why carrier blocklisting matters:

  • Numbers from high-risk regions: Ubisoft's risk engine flags numbers from countries with higher rates of SMS abuse, including Ukraine, Indonesia, Nigeria, and parts of Southeast Asia. If your carrier routes SMS through these, your code might never reach you.

  • Prepaid and VoIP numbers: Ubisoft views prepaid SIMs and VoIP numbers, such as Google Voice and Skype numbers, as higher risk. Carrier filtering is almost guaranteed here.

  • Network congestion. During peak hours, carriers sometimes drop non-critical SMS messages, such as verification codes. Testing after midnight local time often works better.

  • Number recycling: If your current number was previously used by someone else with a Ubisoft account, the system silently rejects it. This is the most common cause of not receiving codes with zero error messages.

Ubisoft uses carrier risk-scoring similar to banking: prepaid VoIP numbers fail at a rate 3x higher than standard mobile numbers.

#Device and App Settings That Block Ubisoft Verification Code Delivery

Honestly? Your phone might be the problem. Android's SMS filtering, Apple's Hide My Number, and third-party call-blocking apps can quietly intercept Ubisoft SMS codes. Do Not Disturb and low-power modes can also delay or completely block delivery.

Check these settings in order:

  • On iOS:

  • Settings > Messages > Filter Unknown Senders → Turn OFF.

  • Settings > Phone > Silence Unknown Callers → Turn OFF.

  • Check your Messages app for an Unknown & Spam folder. Ubisoft codes sometimes hide there.

  • On Android:

  • Open Google Messages > Menu > Spam & Blocked → Look for Ubisoft codes.

  • Settings > Apps > Messages > Notifications → Make sure Allow notification delivery is ON.

  • Third-party blockers:

  • Truecaller, Hiya, and similar apps love classifying verification SMS as spam. Disable SMS filtering temporarily.

  • Airplane mode reset:

  • Turn on Airplane Mode for 30 seconds, then turn it off again. This forces your phone to reconnect, often triggering delayed SMS delivery.

  • Do Not Disturb:

  • DND blocks notifications but not receipts. However, Focus Mode can pause SMS entirely.

A single 'Filter Unknown Senders' toggle on iOS can block up to 90% of SMS verification codes from shortcodes.

#Ubisoft 2FA Code Delay vs. Complete Failure: How to Tell the Difference

A delayed code still arrives within 5–15 minutes; a complete failure never arrives. If you see a code sent but nothing after 20 minutes, your SMS has been blocked at the network level. If you enter an invalid phone number, your number will be blocked.

How to diagnose:

Code sent, but no SMS after 20 minutes. Carrier filtering or network block. Try a different phone number or switch to an authenticator app.

Invalid phone number error. Number blocked by Ubisoft. Use a fresh, never-shared virtual number.

SMS arrives after 15+ minutes Carrier delay or network congestion Wait and enter immediately; future requests may be faster off-peak

Too many attempts error. Recent code requests within 5 minutes. Wait 10 minutes, then request once only.

No error, no SMS, repeated requests. The number is recycled or used on another Ubisoft account. Replace the number permanently.

If your Ubisoft SMS arrives more than 20 minutes after you requested it, the code has likely expired. Ubisoft codes expire in 10 minutes. Request a new one immediately after you receive it.

#The Problem with Disposable Numbers for Ubisoft Account Security Codes

Let's be real: Ubisoft actively blocks most free, online-received, and recycled SMS numbers because they're linked to multi-account abuse. A truly disposable number, brand-new and unique to your session, can bypass these blocklists. But standard free SMS sites? Their number pools are overused and flagged almost immediately.

Why disposable numbers fail so often:

  • Ubisoft cross-references phone numbers against known burner databases. If your number appears on any public SMS receiving website, it's already blocked.

  • Numbers shared publicly are blocked within hours of being listed. Free SMS sites recycle numbers across hundreds of users within minutes.

  • Quality matters: A fresh, one-time-use virtual number from a provider that never recycles has a 95%+ pass rate.

  • Long-term rental numbers avoid the overused tag because they aren't returned to a pool for others to use.

#Ubisoft Account Recovery SMS Failure: Step-by-Step Recovery Without the Code

If the account recovery SMS fails, Ubisoft offers a backup code option if you saved it during setup. Otherwise, use their automated ticket system with your exact account creation date, linked email, and purchase history. For ongoing access, setting up an authenticator app like Google Authenticator avoids SMS dependency entirely.

Step-by-step recovery without SMS:

  • Check for backup codes. Look in Ubisoft account settings > Security > 2FA > Backup Codes. Also, check your email. Ubisoft may have sent them during initial setup.

  • Submit a recovery ticket. Go to support.ubisoft, select Account Recovery, and provide:

  • Exact account creation date

  • Linked email address

  • Last known purchase game title, date, platform

  • Your full name on the account

  • If you regain access:

  • Disable SMS 2FA immediately

  • Set up an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator.

  • Save the new backup codes offline, on paper, or in a password manager.

  • If recovery fails, Ubisoft Support will ask for additional proof of government ID for high-value accounts. Expect 24–72 hours for resolution.

Ubisoft account recovery via ticket takes 1–3 business days. Save your backup codes now to avoid this delay.
