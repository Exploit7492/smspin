#Why Isn't My Uber OTP Arriving? (The Top 5 Culprits)

When the Uber OTP not working issue pops up, it usually traces back to one of three things: your carrier blocking the SMS shortcode, a network delay, or Uber's servers timing out. Carriers like T-Mobile and Verizon are notorious for blocking 5-and 6-digit numbers. And if you've requested codes too many times in a short window, Uber will throttle your account silently.

  • Carrier Shortcode Block: Check if your carrier blocks shortcodes (happens a lot with T-Mobile, Verizon, Vodafone).

  • SIM Swap Lag: If you just switched SIM cards, SMS routing may temporarily fail.

  • Uber Rate Limiting: Requesting more than 3 codes in 5 minutes often triggers a silent block with no error message.

Carrier blocklists cause about 70% of all Uber OTP failures not your phone or the app itself.

#Quick Fixes for Uber SMS Verification Failed (Phone-Based Issues)

Start simple: restart your phone, toggle Airplane Mode for 30 seconds, then force-close and reopen the Uber app. That alone clears up a surprising number of local SMS glitches. Then turn off any SMS filtering apps like Truecaller or Textra they often eat shortcodes without telling you.

  • Force Close + Airplane Mode: Clears the SMS cache and forces a fresh connection.

  • Disable Third-Party SMS Blockers: Apps that block spam can also block legitimate verification codes.

  • Check the SMS Center Number: On Android, dial *#*#4636#*#* to verify the SMS center number isn't blank if it is, contact your carrier.

A simple restart fixes about 30% of Uber SMS verification failed cases.

#The Uber Verification Code Not Sent Server Glitch (What to Do)

Sometimes the issue isn't your phone or carrier it's Uber's SMS gateway timing out during a busy period or regional outage. If you see the verification code not sent message but your phone works fine, wait exactly 60 minutes, then try again. Check Downdetector to see if others report the same problem.

  • Use a Different Login Method: Tap Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple ID to skip SMS entirely.

  • Clear Uber Cache (iOS/Android): Go to Settings > Storage > Clear Cache for the Uber app.

  • Switch to the Uber Eats App: Sometimes verification triggers from a sibling app worth a shot.

For more context on common verification issues, read our blog post.

#How to Request a New Uber Code (Without Getting Locked Out)

You can tap Resend Code, but you must wait at least 60 seconds between tries. Requesting more than 3 times in 10 minutes will lock you out for a full 24 hours. A smarter move? After two failed SMS attempts, look for the Call Me button Uber may offer an automated voice call instead.

  • The Call Me Trick: Keep tapping Resend until the option appears (usually after two failed SMS attempts).

  • Add a Backup Number: Go into your Uber settings and add a second phone number.

  • Wait Window: 24-hour lockouts reset at midnight UTC, so plan accordingly.

Requesting more than 3 codes in under 10 minutes triggers a 24-hour account lockout.

#Why Your Uber Confirmation Code Is Missing Right Now (Network & SMS Blocklists)

Your carrier may have added Uber's shortcode to a spam blocklist more common than you'd think. Carriers like Verizon, T-Mobile, and Vodafone often block commercial shortcodes by default. Also, if your number was used for spam in the past, Uber's risk system might flag it, so the code never actually gets sent.

  • Carrier Blacklist Check: Call your carrier's support and ask them to allow shortcodes 892238 or 656565.

  • Number Reputation Score: Numbers with past spam reports get lower delivery priority.

  • Regional SMS Filtering: Some countries (e.g., India and the UAE) actively filter foreign shortcodes.

People trip up on the small stuff: wrong country code (+1 for US, +44 for UK, +49 for Germany), using a VoIP number (Google Voice, Skype Uber blocks those 100%), or reusing the same virtual number for multiple accounts. Uber tracks usage, so a number used 3+ times may get blocked. Also, turn off your VPN when requesting the code some IP addresses trigger SMS suspension.

  • VPN Conflicts: Disable your VPN while requesting the OTP.
