#Why Your Amazon Verification Code Is Not Received   Common Causes

Let's cut to the chase. Your Amazon verification code is vanishing for one of these reasons: carrier filtering, network delays, international SMS restrictions, or your inbox just being too full to notice. And here's the kicker many free or recycled virtual numbers get instantly blacklisted by Amazon's fraud systems. Travelling abroad? Your home carrier might block shortcode SMS entirely.

  • Carrier filtering: Your mobile provider sometimes flags Amazon shortcodes as spam. Yes, even the legit ones.

  • International roaming: Outside your home country? Shortcode SMS often gets blocked at the network level before it reaches you.

  • Number blacklisting: Free or recycled virtual numbers? Amazon's security algorithms flag them immediately.

  • Inbox full or SIM card issues: A stuffed SMS inbox or a slightly damaged SIM can silently drop incoming messages.

  • App-specific quirks: Amazon's 2FA system sometimes fails to resend or queues codes in ways that make zero sense.

Most Amazon SMS failures are temporary carrier-side blocks, not account problems. A clean virtual number solves this in under 30 seconds.

#Amazon One-Time Password Not Sending? Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Follow this exact order: confirm your phone number is correct, request a new code, wait 90 seconds, then try again. Still nothing? Check if your number format includes the right country code. For international users, forgetting +1 (US/CA) or +44 (UK) guarantees failure every time.

  • Verify the number in account settings: Go to Your Account > Login & Security > Advanced Security Settings.

  • Country code matters: Amazon expects full E.164 format +1 for Canada, +44 for the UK or it just drops the SMS without warning.

  • Try a different device: Got a second phone lying around? Test receiving codes there to isolate device-specific issues.

  • Check Do Not Disturb mode: DND blocks all shortcode SMS on many carriers (especially in India and the Philippines).

  • Wait 5 minutes between retries: Amazon rate-limits OTP requests. Spamming the button can trigger a 24-hour block.

Over 80% of Amazon OTP delivery failures are caused by incorrect phone number formatting or carrier DND settings.

#Amazon Login OTP Problem   What Your Phone or Carrier Might Be Blocking

If you're on Android, check if your SMS app has Spam Protection enabled it often silently quarantines Amazon codes. Carriers like T-Mobile (US), Rogers (Canada), and EE (UK) are notorious for heavy shortcode filtering. And if you're in a restrictive country, your government might be blocking inbound international SMS entirely.

  • Carrier-level blocking: Some providers block all shortcodes by default (common in the Middle East and parts of Africa).

  • SMS app spam filters: Google Messages, Samsung Messages, and third-party apps like Textra may hide shortcodes without telling you.

  • SIM card registration: In India and Indonesia, unregistered SIMs can't receive international SMS including Amazon OTPs.

  • Two-factor authentication lock: If you enabled 2FA via SMS and changed your SIM, old codes will never reach the new number.

  • Blocked numbers list: Check if you accidentally blocked Amazon's sender ID (usually 29203 or 26378).

#Amazon Canada OTP Not Working? Here's the Canada-Specific Fix

Canadian users, listen up: Rogers and Bell networks block shortcodes from US-based senders, and Amazon.ca codes originate from US servers. If your +1 number fails, try a virtual Canadian number that routes through a local SMS provider. Also, make sure your number format is +1 followed by 10 digits any deviation causes silent failure.

  • Rogers shortcode block: Rogers automatically blocks all non-Canadian shortcodes. You can receive SMS instantly with virtual numbers that bypass this.

  • Bell and Telus quirks: Bell sometimes delays SMS by 30–60 seconds. Telus blocks codes after 3 failed resends.

  • Amazon.ca specific: Amazon Canada uses a different shortcode (29203 vs. 26378 for Amazon.com) mismatched codes fail.

  • Provincial differences: Quebec users occasionally face additional carrier proxy issues with one-time codes.

#Amazon UK Verification Code Not Received? Solutions for UK Users

UK users: Amazon.co.uk codes come from a UK shortcode (63503) that works on Vodafone, O2, EE, and Three but only if your phone is on a UK network. Roaming in Europe or using a non-UK eSIM? The SMS gets dropped at the carrier border. A UK-based virtual number resolves this in seconds.

  • EE and Vodafone roaming restrictions: Both carriers block international shortcode forwarding when roaming Amazon UK codes are domestic-only.

  • O2 spam filter: O2's Extra Security feature sometimes intercepts Amazon UK one-time passwords as suspected fraud.

#Common Mistakes That Make Amazon OTP Problems Worse

Most Amazon code issues aren't technical they're user errors. Requesting codes too fast triggers a 24-hour rate limit. Using a number from a free SMS website gets you blacklisted immediately. And trying to verify a new Amazon account with a recycled virtual number always fails because that number is already tied to another account.

  • Rate-limit violation: Requesting more than 3 OTPs in 10 minutes locks your account for 24 hours across all devices.

  • Recycled number trap: Free SMS numbers are used by hundreds of users Amazon automatically rejects them for new account verification.

  • Wrong marketplace: Your Amazon UK account won't accept a +1 Canada OTP you must match the marketplace region.

  • Using SMS for backup 2FA: Lose your SIM, lose Amazon access authenticator apps are safer.

  • Ignoring carrier-specific issues: Assuming call your carrier applies to every phone some (like Google Fi) don't offer shortcode support.
