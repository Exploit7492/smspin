Deliveroo SMS not delivered? Learn how to fix missing OTP codes, carrier filtering, phone settings, number issues, SMS delays, network problems, and alternative verification options.

If you're reading this, you've probably just been staring at your phone, watching the Resend code timer tick past one minute, then three, then five, and nothing's arrived. You're not alone, and here's the thing: this is almost always fixable in under five minutes.

Deliveroo's verification hiccups very rarely start with Deliveroo itself. The real culprit is usually somewhere in the delivery chain: your carrier's spam filters, your phone's SMS blocking settings, or your SIM's reputation with the international messaging gateways that actually push the text to you.

Whether you're a regular customer trying to get back into your account, a new rider setting up your delivery app, or a restaurant partner getting your tools online, this guide walks you through every fix, from the quick phone checks to the permanent solution of grabbing a clean alternate number that actually works.

Who this is for: Anyone who needs a Deliveroo verification code delivered to a mobile number right now and isn't getting it.

When NOT to use alternate numbers: If you're trying to create multiple accounts, verify payment methods with personal ID documents, or use the platform for anything that bends Deliveroo's terms of service, stop here.

#Quick Answer:

Let's cut through the noise. Here's what's most likely going on:

  • Carrier spam filtering is the #1 cause. Your mobile operator silently blocks or delays SMS from global sender IDs they don't recognize. It's annoying, but it's automatic.

  • Check your phone's spam folder first. Both Android and iPhone shunt unknown sender messages into a separate inbox without telling you. It's probably sitting there, looking at you.

  • If nothing arrives in 5 minutes, stop retrying. Hammering Resend on the same SIM can trigger temporary cooldowns, making things worse.

  • The fastest reliable fix is a clean alternate number. A temporary number with no spam history accepts the OTP instantly. It's the nuclear option, but it works.

  • Switching numbers doesn't reset your account. You keep your orders, login, and wallet balance. It just changes where the code lands.

#Why Is Deliveroo Not Sending My Verification SMS? Common Root Causes

Deliveroo sends OTPs through global SMS aggregators, think of them as middlemen between the app and your carrier. Your carrier may silently block or delay messages from senders it doesn't recognize, often because they use international sender IDs, shortcodes, or generic numbers. The most common culprits are your carrier's spam filter, your phone's built-in SMS blocker, an active ad-blocker or VPN, or a mismatch between your account's country code and your SIM's actual home network.

Here's how these usually play out:

  • Carrier-level filtering: Many US, UK, and EU carriers flag high-volume SMS senders. They don't reject the message outright, they delay it by 5–20 minutes. That feels like not arriving, but it's just your carrier holding it ransom.

  • App-level blockers: Both Android Messages spam folder and iOS Filter Unknown Senders can quietly divert OTPs without any notification. You'd never know unless you dig.

  • Sender ID mismatch: If Deliveroo sends from a shortcode your carrier doesn't recognize or actively blocks, the code never surfaces, even though it shows as sent on Deliveroo's end. Classic miscommunication.

  • SIM country code vs. account region: Deliveroo ties verification to your SIM's home country. If you're traveling or using a roaming SIM, codes may route back to your home network and never reach your phone. International travel and OTPs don't mix well.

The Federal Communications Commission's guide on unwanted texts explains how carriers implement legitimate filtering mechanisms, which helps explain why this happens at the network level, not the app level.

#How Long Does Deliveroo SMS Verification Take? Realistic Delivery Times

In normal conditions, Deliveroo OTP messages may arrive fairly quickly, but delivery can vary depending on the carrier, network, and SMS routing. If you've already waited several minutes, continuing to refresh your inbox may not resolve the issue. The message could be delayed, filtered, or routed incorrectly.

  • Actual delivery window: SMS delivery times can vary, and there is no guaranteed arrival time. If the code has not arrived after several minutes, check your phone and carrier settings before requesting another one.

  • Timeout behavior: Deliveroo's Send again option typically starts another verification attempt. If the original message was filtered or delayed, requesting another code may not solve the underlying delivery problem.

  • What 5 minutes means: Waiting a little longer can help with temporary carrier delays, but if the code still has not arrived, review possible filtering, number formatting, network, or routing issues.

Don't spend 20 minutes repeatedly refreshing your inbox. Follow the fixes below, and if your number still has trouble receiving verification messages, consider using free numbers where Deliveroo supports them and its terms allow.

#Deliveroo SMS Not Delivered Within Minutes? Quick Fixes to Try First

Start with the five-minute rule, then run through three quick checks: toggle airplane mode, reboot, and check your Spam/SMS filters. These clear local blocks in under 90 seconds each. No technical degree required.

  • Toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds; this forces your phone to re-register with the carrier network and clear temporary routing glitches. It's the IT crowd's favorite move for a reason.

  • Check your Messages app's Spam/Blocked folder, especially on Android, where OTPs from unknown global senders often land silently. It's probably been there the whole time, patiently waiting.

  • Reboot the phone, not just the app. A hard reset clears the SMS stack cleanly. Yes, "turn it off and on again" applies here too.

  • Turn off VPN and ad-blockers temporarily. These rarely interfere with SMS delivery directly, but they can affect the Deliveroo app's connection and delay the trigger. Worth a shot while you're testing.

If none of these work, don't loop back through the same checks. Request a fresh code once more, and if it still fails, proceed directly to requesting an alternate number. Repeated retries on the same SIM flag your account as suspicious, and a temporary cooldown is the last thing you need right now.

#Deliveroo Verification Code Not Received? Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Android, iPhone, Web

Follow these steps in order. Stop as soon as one resolves the issue.

  • Confirm your SIM has signal, not just Wi-Fi calling mode—airplane mode off, cellular data on. The basics matter.

  • Disable SMS blocking on Android: Settings → Messages → Spam protection → uncheck Block unknown senders. Also check Advanced → SMS filtering → disable for third-party apps.

  • Disable SMS filtering on iPhone: Settings → Messages → Filter Unknown Senders → toggle OFF. Also peek at Settings → Phone → Silence Unknown Callers → toggle OFF. It doesn't affect SMS directly but sometimes bundles with messaging filters.

  • Request one fresh code, not a retry loop. A single resend, max. One shot, one opportunity.

  • Verify your country code in the Deliveroo app profile matches your SIM's home country. A mismatch here explains a lot.

  • SIM swap test: Put your same SIM into another phone. If the code arrives there, your phone's SMS stack is misconfigured, not the routing. Your phone is the problem, not your number.

For Deliveroo web and desktop: Web verification uses a different code pathway. If SMS fails, try the Call me instead option, available in some regions, or contact chat support to verify via email. Don't get stuck in one lane.

If all local checks pass and the code still doesn't arrive, your number is carrier-flagged, and you need an alternate number. For a broader understanding of how SMS verification works across services, see this SMS verification overview.

If your carrier is filtering your primary SIM, using a clean, untouched number fixes the issue in most cases. This works because the problem isn't Deliveroo's server, it's your SIM's spam reputation. That reputation degrades over time from too many verification SMS from various services. It's like a credit score for your phone number, minus the rewards program.

  • When to switch: If you've tried all local fixes and still no code after 15 minutes, switch. Don't burn more time acknowledging the problem.

  • Best practice: Use a number that hasn't been used for other delivery-app verification. Clean reputation equals higher instant acceptance. Makes sense, right?

#Deliveroo SMS Not Delivered? Use a Different SIM: Global Carrier Workarounds Banking, VoIP, and Porting

If you'd rather stay on a physical SIM, switching to a different one, second SIM, travel SIM, or a family member's spare SIM, is a viable fix, provided that SIM has a clean SMS history and can receive global texts. It's the low-tech approach that still works fine.

  • Why VoIP fails: Deliveroo uses premium SMS routes, and VoIP numbers, Google Voice, Skype, and others, are often detected as non-mobile. Deliveroo proactively blocks them at the gateway, resulting in false not-delivered errors. So if you're relying on Google Voice, that's your answer.

  • Second SIM best practices: Turn on the second SIM's data, not just Wi-Fi calling. Some carrier filtering behavior changes when a SIM has been idle for long periods. Keep it active.

  • Roaming caveat: If you're abroad, Deliveroo sometimes requires the SIM's home country to match the app's region. A roaming SIM from the same country works fine, but avoid international roaming if possible, carrier filters get stricter. It's like they don't trust travelers.

  • Porting risk: If you recently ported your number, wait 24–48 hours before requesting a Deliveroo OTP. Your new port's spam reputation reset can cause flaky deliveries. Patience is a virtue here.

#Prevent Future Deliveroo OTP Fails: 3 Settings to Adjust Now

Most future failures are preventable with three baseline hygiene practices. Set these once, and stop worrying about it.

  • Number activity: If your SIM has been inactive for a long period, check that it can send and receive regular SMS before starting verification. Sending a normal text can help confirm that the line is active.

#Deliveroo SMS Verification Waiting Time: What Actually Happens Behind the Scenes

When you hit Send Code, Deliveroo's backend sends it to a global SMS gateway, which routes based on your SIM's network identifiers, not just the displayed number. This is why wait time varies by carrier. It's not magic; it's routing logic.

  • The 3–5 second window: OTP gateways request delivery confirmation. Actual delivery receipts can be carrier-dependent, but routing decisions are made in under 10 seconds. After that, no automatic retry happens. Once it's decided, it's decided.

  • Why waiting doesn't help: If your carrier tagged the sender as spam, your SIM won't receive it, no matter how much time passes. The ignore list expires after 24–48 hours for delivery apps, not minutes. Waiting won't change the verdict.

  • Deliveroo's resend mechanics: The Resend button forces a new SMS request, not a queued retry. Success depends entirely on your SIM's current filter state, not on timing since the last request: same path, same outcome.

  • What you can control: Your SIM's reputation, your SMS history with other services, and the carrier's spam policy, both beyond Deliveroo's control. So alternate solutions, like clean numbers, are often faster. Control what you can, and let go of what you can't.

#When All Else Fails: Deliveroo Support Escalation Path What to Say, What to Expect

If you've tried alternate numbers and other SIMs, and still nothing, contact Deliveroo support directly through in-app chat, not email, as response times are faster. Go straight to the source.

  • What to say: My SMS verification code isn't arriving on my phone. I've tried an alternate number and still nothing. What is the expected delivery time for my region and carrier? This sets expectations you can debug, rather than a generic "help me" attempt. Specific beats vague.

  • What to include: Your region, country, carrier, and number type, physical SIM or virtual. Support can check carrier routes specific to your region and explain why local blocks may happen. Give them what they need to help you.

  • What NOT to say: Avoid saying I used a temporary number. Some support agents will confuse this with fraud concerns. Say I used an alternate number in the same region if troubleshooting requires it. Discretion is the better part of valor here.

  • Expected outcome: Best case, support sends a compensation code or verifies via your email pathway instead. They may ask you to wait 24 hours for the carrier cool-off period, then retry with a new SIM number. Typical resolution: within one business day via chat, not days via email. That's a reasonable timeline.

#Frequently Asked Questions:

Is it legal to use a temporary number for Deliveroo verification?

Yes, as long as you're verifying your own legitimate account and you're not violating any service terms. Deliveroo's rules apply to how you use the account, so make sure your verification method is permitted. SMSPin requires you to follow app terms and local regulations.

Why does my Deliveroo code never arrive even after multiple tries?

Your carrier may be filtering automated or international SMS as spam. Repeated retries on the same number may not resolve the issue. Check your SMS settings, wait before requesting another code, and contact your carrier or Deliveroo support if the problem continues.

What's the difference between a one time number and a rental number for Deliveroo?

One time numbers are intended for a single verification. Rental numbers remain active for a longer period, from days to a month, and can be useful when you need continued access to the same number for legitimate verification purposes.

What should I NOT use a temp number for?

Don't use temporary numbers to create multiple unauthorized accounts or verify payment methods linked to your personal identity. These activities can violate Deliveroo's terms of service and may lead to account restrictions.

I tried everything. What's my last resort?

Wait before requesting another verification code and check whether your carrier is blocking automated SMS. If the issue continues, contact Deliveroo support or your carrier for assistance. If you choose to use a temporary number, make sure it is permitted for your account and verification method and follow the applicable terms and regulations.
