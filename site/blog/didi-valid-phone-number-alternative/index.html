#Why Is My DiDi Verification Code Not Arriving?

A missing DiDi OTP usually isn't a platform-wide meltdown. It's typically something specific to your carrier, device, or network setup. The usual suspects: a full SMS inbox, an overzealous spam filter, or your mobile operator quietly blocking shortcode messages from international senders.

Let's break down what's actually happening behind the scenes so you can skip the guesswork.

  • Carrier Filtering: Many US and UK carriers block traffic from shortcodes those 5–6 digit numbers that aren't allowed especially from ride-hailing apps registered in other regions.

  • Inbox Full: When your SMS storage hits 100%, new messages bounce back before they ever reach you.

  • Do Not Disturb DND: Both Android and iOS can suppress notifications in DND mode, making it look like the code never arrived.

  • Network Glitch: A brief signal drop like switching from 4G to 3G can cause the SMS gateway to time out.

Sound familiar? The fixes below should help.

#Quick Fixes for DiDi SMS OTP Delivery Problems

Before you look for a new number, run through these five quick fixes. They can help identify common causes of SMS delivery failures, including network issues, blocked messages, and resend restrictions.

The fastest fix is to restart your phone. This refreshes the device's connection to the mobile network. If that doesn't help, toggle airplane mode on and then off to reconnect to the network.

Here's the full sequence:

  • Restart your phone: Refreshes the device's network connection and may resolve temporary connectivity issues.

  • Toggle airplane mode: Turn it on briefly, then turn it off to reconnect to the mobile network.

  • Check your blocked list: Open your SMS app settings and make sure DiDi's sender ID or number hasn't been blocked.

  • Wait before requesting a new code: Repeated resend requests may trigger temporary restrictions. Follow the waiting period shown in the app.

  • Test with another service: If appropriate, check whether you can receive SMS from another service to help determine whether the issue is specific to DiDi or your carrier.

If none of these steps work, the problem may involve your carrier, the number type, or DiDi's verification system. Check the platform's requirements before trying another supported number.

#Troubleshooting DiDi Verification Text Not Received on iOS and Android.

Your operating system handles SMS messages differently, so the fix often depends on whether you're using an iPhone or an Android device. On iOS, Filter Unknown Senders may move the DiDi verification message into a separate tab. On Android, Spam Protection may automatically categorize it as junk.

Here's where to look:

On iOS iPhone:

  • Open Messages and tap Filters in the top-left corner, then select Unknown Senders.

  • Look for the DiDi verification message. If you find it, move it back to your main inbox if that option is available.

  • Check Settings → Notifications → Messages to make sure notifications are enabled.

On Android Messages:

  • Open Messages, tap the three-dot menu, and select Spam & Blocked.

  • If the DiDi code is in the Spam folder, open the message and select Not spam if the option is available.

  • Check your battery settings, as aggressive battery optimization may affect background activity. Make sure the Messages app can operate normally.

Dual SIM pitfall: If you use two SIMs, check that you're viewing messages for the same number you entered in the DiDi app. It's easy to overlook a code received on the other SIM.

If the code still doesn't arrive, check your carrier connection and DiDi's current verification requirements. For legitimate verification needs, SMSPin may provide alternative number options where DiDi supports the number type and its use complies with DiDi's requirements.

If your main number is blocked, hammering the resend button will only produce more "code not received" errors.

Here's what might be happening behind the scenes:
