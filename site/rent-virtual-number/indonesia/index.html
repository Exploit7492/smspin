Indonesia rental FAQ

Is it legal to use an Indonesian virtual number?

Yes, it is legal as long as you use it for legitimate purposes, such as account verification or privacy protection. You must follow the terms of service of the app you are signing up for and local regulations. SMSPin does not support fraud or illegal activity.

Why does my code sometimes fail to arrive?

Codes can fail due to app-side blocks, temporary number exhaustion, or network delays. If this happens, try a different number from the same pool. SMSPin automatically refunds your payment if no SMS is delivered so that you can try again without risk.

What’s the difference between a one-time number and a rental number?

A one-time number is used for a single SMS verification and expires once the code is received. A rental number stays active for days or weeks (1, 7, or 30 days), perfect for ongoing use like a business WhatsApp account. Rentals cost more upfront but are cheaper for repeated use.

Can I use a virtual number for WhatsApp or Telegram?

Absolutely. Indonesian virtual numbers work seamlessly with WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, and hundreds of other services. Just ensure you select the correct service when renting the number, as some apps have specific number pools.

What should I NOT use a temporary number for?

Do not use virtual numbers for fraud, spam, or other activity that breaks a platform's rules to scam others, or violating any app's terms of service. They are intended for privacy protection, legitimate account verification, and app testing only.

How do I troubleshoot a delayed or missing SMS?

First, wait 60 seconds and refresh your dashboard. If still no code, re-request the SMS from the app. If it still fails, request a refund and try a different number from the same service. Most issues are resolved by switching numbers.

Will my rental number be reused by someone else?

Yes, after your rental period ends, the number is returned to the pool and can be assigned to another user. It is not permanently yours. For sensitive accounts, avoid using a rental number for long-term logins.
