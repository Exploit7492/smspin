Cyprus rental FAQ

Is it legal to rent a virtual phone number in Cyprus?

Yes, renting a virtual number for legitimate purposes, such as protecting your privacy or testing apps, is legal. The problem arises only if you use it for fraud, spam, or violating an app's terms. Always check the app's policies first.

Why do some OTP codes fail with Cyprus virtual numbers?

Common reasons include the app blocking all virtual numbers, the specific number being previously flagged, or a temporary carrier delay in Cyprus. Try a fresh number from a provider that tests app acceptance, and wait up to 10 minutes. If it still fails, request a refund.

What's the difference between a one-time virtual number and a rented number?

One-time numbers are single-use; they work for one verification and then expire. Rented numbers remain active for days or weeks, allowing you to receive multiple SMS codes over time. Choose one-time for quick sign-ups, rental for long-term access or account recovery.

What should I NOT use a Cyprus virtual number for?
