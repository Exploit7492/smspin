Why did my OTP code fail to arrive?

Common causes include the app blocking virtual numbers, the number being recycled from a banned user, or a temporary pipeline issue. Release the number and rent a fresh one. If it fails again, the app likely doesn't support virtual numbers for that country.

What's the difference between a one-time rental and a monthly rental?

One-time rentals are ideal for a single verification and expire quickly. Monthly rentals keep the same number active for up to 30 days, which is useful for apps like WhatsApp that may re-verify your number later.

Can I use a Bahamas virtual number for banking or financial services?

Many banks block virtual numbers for security. Check with your bank first. If they support VoIP or virtual providers, it might work, but expect a higher failure rate.

What should I NOT use a temporary Bahamas number for?
