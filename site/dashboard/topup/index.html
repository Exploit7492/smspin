Frequently asked

Will my balance expire?

No. Your balance never expires and carries over indefinitely until you spend it.

Are there any fees?

No top-up fees. You are only charged for the SMS codes you actually receive.

Can I get an invoice / VAT receipt?

Yes. Every payment generates an invoice in the table above — contact support@smspin.io for a VAT receipt.

Is crypto separate?

Crypto top-ups are credited after 1 network confirmation. Crypto balance is spent the same way as any other funds.

What if a payment fails?

Failed payments are never charged. If a charge fails after authorisation, it is reversed automatically within a few days.
