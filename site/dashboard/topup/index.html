Frequently asked
Will my balance expire?

No. Your balance never expires and carries over indefinitely until you spend it.

Which payment methods can I use?

Debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve) and instant NGN bank transfer, both handled securely by KoraPay. The amount you top up is charged in Nigerian Naira (₦).

Are there any fees?

A flat 2.5% processing fee applies to every top-up; the summary above shows exactly what reaches your wallet. You are then only charged for the SMS codes you actually receive.

How fast is my balance credited?

Card and bank-transfer payments are credited automatically the moment KoraPay confirms them — usually within seconds. You never need to send us a receipt.

Can I get an invoice / VAT receipt?

Yes. Every payment generates an invoice in the table above — contact support@smspin.io for a VAT receipt.

What if a payment fails?

Failed payments are never charged. If a charge fails after authorisation, it is reversed automatically within a few days.
